Before you ask what if Germany won World War 2, ask why every empire that big died anyway.
But first, the how. Germany made three huge mistakes.
It invaded the Soviet Union in June 1941, then stalled into a Russian winter.
It let about 338,000 trapped soldiers escape Dunkirk.
And four days after Pearl Harbor, it declared war on the United States.
So flip all three. Crush the Soviets before the snow. Hit Britain before it regroups. Leave America alone.
Ireland stays neutral, so Britain fights alone.
And America? In 1939, 20,000 people packed Madison Square Garden for a pro-Nazi rally. Staying out was a real option.
So the Reich grows from this to this.
Now what?
Look at Rome. Biggest in 117 AD. Too big to hold, too many people forced under one flag.
By 395 it split in two. The West fell in 476. The East limped on for a thousand years.
You can't stamp one eagle on every passport and expect peace. And a regime built on mass murder holds people by fear, not loyalty.
So the best case? The Reich splits. East and West.
One thrives, one rots. Same old imperial cycle.
The rotting half breaks into countries again, they gang up on the other half, and it loses its Lebensraum too.
In the end, Germany is right back where it started.
Oh well. Time for World War 3.
