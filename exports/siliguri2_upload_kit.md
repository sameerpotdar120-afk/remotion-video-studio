## Fact notes

See `public/siliguri2/script_draft.md` and `reference_analysis.md` (checked 18 November 2026, with sources):
- About 22 km at the narrowest point. The map build measures 22.5 km from the Nepal border to the Bangladesh border.
- The 8 Northeast states, about 4.5 crore people.
- Doklam: 16 June to 28 August 2017, 73 days.
- Lalmonirhat: a 1931 airfield; its revival was announced in 2025.
- The "Greater Bangladesh" map came from a Dhaka group, not the government. It was the subject of a Rajya Sabha reply
  on 1 August 2025.
- No. 101 Squadron has flown Rafales at Hasimara since 2021.
- S-400: reported, not official.
- Three garrisons were reported in November 2025.
