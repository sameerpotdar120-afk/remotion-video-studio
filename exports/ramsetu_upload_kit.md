## Fact notes (checked 13 November 2026)

- **"रामेश्वरम मंदिर के रिकॉर्ड कहते हैं, 1480 तक":** the temple records' account of a 1480 storm; Britannica also says
  records suggest it was passable on foot until 1480. A record, not proof; the VO credits it to the records.
- **"धनुषकोडी… धनुष का सिरा… धनुष की नोक से सेतु को तोड़ा":** the traditional legend behind the name (dhanush + kodi,
  the end or tip of the bow).
- **"वाल्मीकि रामायण में इसे नल ने बनाया… विश्वकर्मा के बेटे नल… पाँच दिन":** Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda: Nala,
  son of Vishwakarma, builds the setu in five days. Floating stones and Ram naam come from later tradition (Ramcharitmanas,
  folk accounts); the VO says "कहते हैं".
- **"अंग्रेज़ों के नक्शों में… Adam's Bridge":** the name used on British maps from the early 1800s.
- **"2024 में ISRO… NASA के satellite के laser… 29 km… 99.98%… करीब 8 मीटर":** Dandabathula et al., Scientific Reports
  14:14896 (2024), ISRO NRSC, from NASA's ICESat-2 laser data: about 29 km from Dhanushkodi to Talaimannar, 99.98%
  submerged, the ridge about 8 m above the seabed.
- **"चूना-पत्थर और रेत के टीले… धनुषकोडी और मन्नार का ही समुंदर के नीचे वाला हिस्सा":** the same study (a submarine
  continuation of Dhanushkodi and Talaimannar).
- **"22 दिसंबर 1964… 115… 1,800":** the cyclone of 22-23 December 1964; the Pamban-Dhanushkodi passenger train was
  washed away with about 115 people aboard; about 1,800 died on Pamban island; the town was declared unfit to live in.
