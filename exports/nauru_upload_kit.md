## Fact notes (checked 22–23 Nov 2026)

- Nauru 21 km²; Delhi IGI airport 2,066 ha ≈ 20.66 km² (one outlet says 6,300 acres, hence "जितना", about the same). In the
  video Nauru's coastline is traced from the Sentinel-2 image and laid over the airport at true size.
- Third-smallest country by area (after Vatican City and Monaco). Independence 1968.
- 1975 per-capita GDP ≈ $50,000, second only to Saudi Arabia: Helen Hughes' estimate. Others give $35,700. The US figure was about
  $7,800, so "more than America" holds either way; the video says "एक अंदाज़े के हिसाब से" and labels the bars "अनुमान".
- Air Nauru, 1983: seven jets (two 727s, five 737s) for about 8,000 people. 8,000 / 7 ≈ 1,100 per jet; a Delhi–Patna flight
  carries about 180–190, so about six full flights.
- Phosphate from seabird droppings: the common account, hence "माना जाता है".
- More than 80% of the island devastated: 1987 Commission of Inquiry. Near bankrupt by about 2000.
- Australia: hosts Australia's offshore processing (since 2012) and, under a 2025 deal, takes people Australia deports after
  releasing them from immigration detention: about US$1.62B (A$2.5B) over 30 years.
- Citizenship by investment: launched at $105,000; today's standard contribution is $115,000 plus fees.
