## Fact notes

- "भारत ने… पहली बार दाने बनाए": crystallised sugar is first attested in India, around the Gupta period (about the 4th–6th century CE). "करीब 1500 साल पहले" fits that.
- "शर्करा… छोटे कंकड़": Sanskrit śarkarā means gravel or grit. It is the root of Persian shakar, Arabic sukkar, Italian zucchero, French sucre and English sugar.
- "खण्ड… कंद… Candy": Sanskrit khaṇḍa (piece) → Persian qand → Arabic qandī → Old French "sucre candi" → English candy. Kalakand and gulkand carry the same "qand".
- "मिस्र… मिश्री": misri comes from Arabic Miṣr (Egypt). Egypt was known for crystal sugar.
- "China से आई सफ़ेद शक्कर… चीनी": "chini" is linked to white refined sugar associated with China. The script deliberately doesn't claim China invented refining.
- The map draws India with its official boundary (Natural Earth's India point-of-view edition).
