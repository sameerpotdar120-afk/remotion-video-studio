## Fact notes

- **"111 गाँव… 162 टुकड़े":** at the 2015 exchange, India transferred 111 enclaves to Bangladesh and received 51. The 111 counts individual mauzas, not geographic enclaves (there were 102 Indian enclaves in mainland Bangladesh by that count). "गाँव" for each piece is the common usage.
- **Dahala Khagrabari:** the world's only third-order enclave, less than 0.7 hectares (about 1.7 acres): an Indian piece inside a Bangladeshi enclave, inside an Indian enclave, inside Bangladesh.
- **"कहते हैं, राजा शतरंज में…":** a popular legend, which is why the line says "कहते हैं" and the screen shows "लोककथा". The real origin is the old land holdings across the Cooch Behar kingdom and Mughal (Rangpur) boundary.
- **"करीब 51,000 लोग":** the 2010 joint headcount found 51,549 residents (37,334 in Indian enclaves, 14,215 in Bangladeshi enclaves).
- **"68 साल":** 1947 to 2015.
- **"31 July 2015 की आधी रात":** the exchange took effect at midnight on 31 July 2015, under the 1974 Land Boundary Agreement, put into force with India's 100th Constitutional Amendment (2015).
- **"करीब 1,000 लोग… भारत आ गए":** about 1,000 residents of the former Indian enclaves chose Indian citizenship and moved to India by the end of November 2015. Reported figures are a little under 1,000, which "करीब" covers.
- **Map:** India is drawn with its official boundary. The enclave dots are a code illustration: real counts, illustrative positions.
