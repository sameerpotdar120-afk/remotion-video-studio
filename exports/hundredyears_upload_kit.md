## Fact notes (checked 19 October 2026)

- **"116 साल तक दो देश लड़ते रहे":** 1337 to 1453 is 116 years. It was a run of wars with long truces in between, not 116 years of non-stop fighting; the name "Hundred Years' War" came later.
- **"17 साल की किसान की बेटी":** Joan was born around 1412 in Domrémy, to a farming family. She reached Charles at Chinon in early 1429, aged about 17.
- **"मेरी माँ France की राजकुमारी थी":** Edward III's mother, Isabella, was the daughter of Philip IV of France. When Charles IV died in 1328 without a son, the French crown went to Philip VI (Valois), not to Edward. Edward's line is a dramatised version of his claim.
- **"बात है 1337 की … France ने मना कर दिया":** in 1337 Philip VI confiscated Gascony from Edward, and Edward pressed his claim to the French throne (he formally took the title King of France in 1340). The video simplifies this into "France said no".
- **"longbow के सामने ये कवच काग़ज़ जैसा":** a figure of speech. English longbowmen beat French mounted knights at Crécy (1346), Poitiers (1356) and Agincourt (1415); much of the damage was to horses and to knights caught in mud and crowds, not arrows punching through plate.
- **"मुझे ऊपर से आवाज़ आई है":** Joan said she heard the voices of Saint Michael, Saint Catherine and Saint Margaret.
- **"1429 में Orléans … तीर लगने के बाद भी":** the siege of Orléans was lifted on 8 May 1429. On 7 May Joan was hit by an arrow between her neck and shoulder and went back to the fight.
- **"Reims में Charles VII का राजतिलक":** 17 July 1429, at Reims Cathedral, with Joan present.
- **"Burgundy के सैनिकों ने उसे पकड़कर England को बेच दिया":** captured at Compiègne on 23 May 1430 by Burgundian forces; handed to the English for 10,000 livres.
- **"Rouen में अदालत … मर्दों के कपड़े":** the trial was a church court held at Rouen under English control. The charges were heresy; going back to men's clothing was the grounds for the "relapse" verdict.
- **"सिर्फ़ 19 साल की उम्र में उसे ज़िंदा जला दिया गया":** burned at the stake in Rouen on 30 May 1431, aged about 19.
- **"Church ने जिसे चुड़ैल कहकर जलवाया":** "चुड़ैल" is the popular framing; the formal verdict was relapsed heretic, not witchcraft.
- **"1453 में France ने ये जंग जीत ली":** the Battle of Castillon (17 July 1453) and the fall of Bordeaux that October ended the war; England kept only Calais (until 1558).
- **"लगभग 500 साल बाद … Saint":** a retrial in 1456 cleared her; she was beatified in 1909 and canonised by Pope Benedict XV on 16 May 1920, 489 years after her death.
