## Fact notes (checked 9 November 2026)

- **"India असल में Greenland से डेढ़ गुना बड़ा", "22 लाख", "33 लाख":** India 3,287,263 km² (official figure), Greenland about 2,166,086 km². Ratio about 1.52.
- **"Greenland ऊपर से नीचे 2,670 km, पूरब से पश्चिम 1,050 km":** the commonly quoted dimensions (about 2,670 km north–south, about 1,050 km east–west). The east–west line is drawn on the parallel where Greenland's width on Natural Earth matches 1,050 km (about 74.8° N); at its very widest, around 77° N across the north-west cape, it is closer to 1,300 km. A sharp commenter may raise this.
- **"India 3,214 km, 2,933 km":** India's official north–south and east–west extents (Government of India figures).
- **"Africa, Greenland से 14 गुना बड़ा":** Africa about 30.37 million km² ÷ Greenland 2.17 million km² ≈ 14.
- **"1569 में Gerardus Mercator":** his world map "Nova et Aucta Orbis Terrae Descriptio…" (Duisburg, 1569). Its point was that a straight line on it is a line of constant compass bearing (a rhumb line).
- **"School की किताबों से लेकर Google Maps के app तक":** Google Maps uses Web Mercator, a close variant of Mercator. On desktop, zooming far out now switches to a 3D globe; at street and city zoom it is still Mercator. The line is fine as said.
- **"Greenland के बीच में area करीब 10 गुना":** Mercator stretches area by 1/cos²(latitude). At about 72° N, the middle of Greenland, that is about 10.5×.
- **"India के पास बस करीब 15%":** at about 22° N, the middle of India, 1/cos²(22°) ≈ 1.16, about +16%. "करीब 15%" is fair.
- **"धरती को छह टुकड़ों में काटो…":** an illustration (an interrupted projection with 6 gores morphing into Mercator), not how Mercator actually built the map. It shows why stretching is unavoidable.
- **True-size move:** Greenland is moved on the sphere (rotated, then reprojected) the way thetruesize.com does it, so its size next to India is the real one.
