## Fact notes (checked 12 November 2026)

- **"भारत हर साल करीब पाँच सेंटीमीटर खिसक रहा है":** the Indian plate moves about 4–5 cm a year to the north-east
  (Geological Society; GPS studies).
- **"करीब तीस करोड़ साल पहले… पैंजिया":** Pangea assembled about 335 million years ago and began to break up about 200
  million years ago, so "about 300 million years ago" sits inside its lifetime.
- **"Africa, Antarctica और Australia से चिपका हुआ":** in Gondwana, India sat between Madagascar/Africa, Antarctica and
  Australia.
- **"दुनिया का सबसे तेज़ भागता टुकड़ा… पंद्रह सेंटीमीटर से भी ज़्यादा":** after leaving Madagascar about 90 million years
  ago, India moved at roughly 15–20 cm a year, the fastest known plate motion of that time.
- **"टेथिस" / "करीब पाँच करोड़ साल पहले… Asia से जा टकराया":** India met Asia about 50–55 million years ago, closing the
  Tethys sea.
- **"Everest की चोटी पर… समंदर के जीवों के fossil":** Everest's summit rock (the Qomolangma Formation) is Ordovician
  marine limestone with fossils of trilobites, crinoids and other sea creatures.
- **"शालिग्राम… ammonite के fossil":** shaligrams are ammonite fossils from the Kali Gandaki river in Nepal, from the
  Jurassic Tethys sea (about 15–20 crore years old; the on-screen tag says "15+ करोड़ साल").
- **"एक model कहता है… Pangaea Ultima":** one model (Scotese) of the next supercontinent; others (Amasia, Novopangaea)
  exist, hence "एक model".
- **"चालीस से सत्तर डिग्री… आठ से सोलह प्रतिशत ज़मीन":** Farnsworth et al., Nature Geoscience 2023: much of the land at
  40–70°C, 8–16% habitable for mammals, about 250 million years from now.
