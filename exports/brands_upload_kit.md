## Fact notes (checked 24 Nov 2026)

- Bata: founded 1894 in Zlín (then Austria-Hungary, now Czech Republic) by Tomáš, Antonín and Anna Baťa. Group HQ today:
  Lausanne, Switzerland, so the video says "Czech Republic से आई". India: incorporated 1931, first unit at Konnagar near
  Kolkata (1931–32), Batanagar from 1934 ("1930s"). Promoter Bata (BN) B.V. held 52.96 % until 2022 and about 50 % since
  (aggregator data: check the latest BSE/NSE shareholding filing before upload; "बड़ा हिस्सा" stays true either way).
- Maggi: Swiss origin, Nestlé (Switzerland). Colgate: Colgate-Palmolive, New York. Lifebuoy: Unilever (UK).
- Louis Philippe: launched 1989 by Madura Garments in India (one source gives 1988 for Madura Garments itself); named
  after Louis-Philippe I, King of France 1830–48; now Aditya Birla.
- Monte Carlo: launched 1984 by Oswal Woollen Mills (Nahar group), Ludhiana.
- Red Tape: Mirza International, founded in Kanpur (1979); the brand dates to 1996. The listed company is now in Noida,
  so the video says "जड़ें कानपुर में".
- Lakmé: started 1952 by Tata Oil Mills, named after Delibes' opera; sold to Hindustan Unilever in the late 1990s, which
  is why the script says "देसी निकलते हैं" and not "100 % देसी".
- On screen: the "≈50 %" ring is labelled "विदेशी parent कंपनी"; the "1989" pill for Louis Philippe is the launch year.
