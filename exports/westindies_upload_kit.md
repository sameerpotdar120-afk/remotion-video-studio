## Fact notes

- "13 आज़ाद देश": the Caribbean has 13 sovereign states (Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Barbados, Cuba, Dominica, Dominican Republic, Grenada, Haiti, Jamaica, St Kitts and Nevis, St Lucia, St Vincent and the Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago). Other islands are territories of the UK, France, the Netherlands and the US.
- Cricket West Indies is made up of six boards: Barbados, Guyana, Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, the Leeward Islands and the Windward Islands, all former British colonies. Cuba, Haiti, the Dominican Republic and the Bahamas are not part of it.
- Guyana is on the South American mainland.
- Columbus landed in the Bahamas in 1492 believing he had reached the Indies; the name West Indies separated them from the East Indies.
- The West Indies Federation ran from 1958 to 1962. Jamaica voted to leave in a September 1961 referendum, and the federation was dissolved in May 1962 (the script rounds this to "4 साल").
