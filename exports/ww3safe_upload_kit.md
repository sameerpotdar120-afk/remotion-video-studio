## Fact notes (checked October 2026)

- **"सारे 9 परमाणु देश … उत्तरी हिस्से में":** the nine nuclear-armed states are the US, Russia, China, France, the UK, India, Pakistan, Israel and North Korea. All lie north of the equator.
- **Fiji, "300 से ज़्यादा द्वीप":** about 330 islands.
- **Fiji, "Australia से करीब 2,700 किलोमीटर":** Nadi to Brisbane is about 2,720 km and Suva to Brisbane about 2,800 km. New Zealand is actually nearer (Auckland to Suva, about 2,110 km); the line doesn't call Australia the nearest.
- **"किसी के निशाने पर आने की वजह ही नहीं बनती":** long-range missiles can reach anywhere, so the line only says Fiji has no reason to be a target.
- **Fiji and the treaty:** the Treaty of Rarotonga (1985) made the South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone, and Fiji is a party.
- **New Zealand, 1987:** the New Zealand Nuclear Free Zone, Disarmament, and Arms Control Act 1987.
- **The 2023 study:** Wilson and Boyd, *Risk Analysis* (University of Otago, February 2023). Under nuclear-winter crop models, Australia, New Zealand, Iceland, the Solomon Islands and Vanuatu could grow enough food for their own people. The authors warn that fuel imports and industrial collapse could still threaten that, so the line says "उगा सकता है".
- **Iceland, "अपनी army नहीं":** no standing army (it has a Coast Guard). It is a founding NATO member, which the video doesn't contradict.
- **Iceland, "19 साल से सबसे शांत":** Global Peace Index 2026 ranks Iceland first, its 19th year in a row (since 2008); one outlet says 18th, most say 19th.
- **Iceland, "90% घर":** Samorka (Iceland's utility association) says 90% of housing is heated directly by geothermal water; older government material says about 85%.
- **The fourth country:** Switzerland. A 2017 federal figure put shelter spaces at 110% of the population; coverage is uneven between cantons, and in 2025 the government set aside 220 million francs to inspect and repair public shelters. The video says the spaces exist ("बनी हुई है"), not that they're all ready.
- **India:** shown with its official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition).
