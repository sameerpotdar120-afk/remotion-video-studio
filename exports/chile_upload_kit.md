## Fact notes (checked October 2026)

- **"करीब 4,300 KM" and "1,000 KM बच जाए":** Britannica gives about 4,300 km from the Peruvian border to Cape Horn (the IAEA gives 4,329 km). India's official north–south extent is 3,214 km, so Chile is about 1,090 km longer.
- **"औसतन 180 KM", "Delhi से Agra":** Britannica gives an average width of about 177 km and the IAEA 180 km. Delhi to Agra is about 180 km in a straight line (the road is longer); the line says "लगभग".
- **"1500 के आसपास … Inca, Mapuche":** the Inca empire reached into northern and central Chile; the Mapuche held the south.
- **"Spain कभी पूरी तरह नहीं जीत पाया":** Araucanía stayed de facto independent through the colonial era (the Arauco War). Chile occupied it in 1861–1883, which the video doesn't cover.
- **"1818 में आज़ाद":** the declaration of independence is dated 12 February 1818; the early republic held only the central part of today's Chile.
- **"ज़मीन की सबसे लंबी पर्वतमाला":** the Andes are generally described as the longest continental mountain range. No length figure is used.
- **"नीचे बढ़ता गया … आख़िरी छोर तक":** through the Strait of Magellan (Fuerte Bulnes, 1843), Araucanía (1861–83) and the 1881 boundary treaty with Argentina.
- **"1879 में Bolivia और Peru से जंग":** the War of the Pacific, 1879–1883/84.
- **The coast:** Chile took Bolivia's whole coast (Antofagasta) and Peru's Tarapacá; Arica became Chilean under the 1929 treaty. Bolivia's loss was confirmed by the 1904 treaty.
- **"Bolivia की Navy … झील और नदियों में":** the Bolivian Naval Force was formed in 1963–66 and operates on Lake Titicaca and on rivers.
- **The skit:** the joke is on the conquistadors, never on the locals; the Mapuche line shows their resistance.
- **India:** shown with its official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition).
