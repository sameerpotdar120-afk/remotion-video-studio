## Fact notes (checked October 2026)

- **"22 किलोमीटर":** most sources give about 20–22 km at the narrowest point. Measured on Natural Earth's borders between Nepal and Bangladesh, the gap on screen is 22.5 km.
- **"साढ़े चार करोड़ लोग", "8 राज्य", "8 प्रतिशत ज़मीन":** the eight northeastern states had about 4.5 crore people in the 2011 census and cover about 8% of India's area.
- **Road, rail, oil:** the corridor is the only land link between mainland India and the Northeast; the routes on screen are drawn approximately.
- **Neighbours and the Chumbi Valley:** the corridor lies between Nepal and Bangladesh with Bhutan to the north; China's Chumbi Valley sits between Sikkim and Bhutan. Its distance to the corridor depends on the point measured, so the video gives none.
- **"2018 के समझौते से":** India and Bangladesh signed the agreement on using Chattogram and Mongla ports for Northeast cargo in October 2018; trial runs began in 2020.
- **Mongla:** under a 2015 India–Bangladesh initiative, land at Mongla was set aside for an Indian economic zone. Bangladesh delisted it in October 2025, and a China Civil Engineering Construction Corporation MoU for a Chinese economic zone on the same 110 acres was signed in June 2026. Separately, Bangladesh signed a $335.78 M China Exim Bank loan in October 2026 to expand Mongla port.
- **Teesta project:** Bangladesh has sought Chinese financing for the Teesta river project (feasibility study by PowerChina); the area is close to the Siliguri Corridor.
- **"Army यहाँ 3 नए ठिकाने बना रही है":** reports from November 2025 name new garrisons at Bamuni (Dhubri, Assam), Kishanganj (Bihar) and Chopra (West Bengal); reports differ on whether they were complete, so the video says "बना रही है".
- **Kaladan Project:** Kolkata to Sittwe by sea, up the Kaladan river to Paletwa, then by road to Mizoram; the port works, and the road section is still being built.
- **Malacca, "2.8 किलोमीटर":** the narrowest point on the Malacca–Singapore route (Phillips Channel) is about 2.8 km wide. "China के तेल का बड़ा हिस्सा" is used instead of the older 80% figure, which is outdated.
- **Andaman-Nicobar "दरवाज़े पर":** the islands lie near the northern entrance of the Strait of Malacca.
- **India:** shown with its official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition).
