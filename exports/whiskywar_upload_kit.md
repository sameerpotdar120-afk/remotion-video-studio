## Fact notes (checked 20 October 2026)

- **"49 साल तक एक जंग":** from the 1973 Canada–Denmark continental-shelf treaty, which left Hans Island undecided, to the 14 June 2022 agreement. It was a friendly dispute, never a shooting war; "जंग" is the popular nickname, the "Whisky War".
- **"इस छोटे से पत्थर के लिए: Hans Island":** an uninhabited limestone island of about 1.2–1.3 km² in the Kennedy Channel of the Nares Strait, between Ellesmere Island (Canada) and Greenland.
- **"1953 में Greenland पूरी तरह Denmark का हिस्सा बन गया":** Denmark's 1953 constitution ended Greenland's colonial status and made it an integral part of the Kingdom. (Greenland has had home rule since 1979 and self-government since 2009.)
- **"दोनों किनारों से करीब 18 किलोमीटर":** the channel is about 35 km wide there, with the island near the middle.
- **"1984 में Canada के सैनिक … Canadian whisky":** Canadian soldiers planted a flag and left a bottle of Canadian whisky in 1984.
- **"उसी साल … Tom Høyem":** Denmark's Minister for Greenland, Tom Høyem, visited later in 1984 (28 July is given for the flag), raised the Danish flag and left a bottle of schnapps with a note, "Welcome to the Danish Island". The reference video tells it Denmark-first; the order in our video follows the sources.
- **"सैनिक फिर आए, झंडा फिर बदला":** the two sides kept swapping flags and bottles on visits for years; it is a well-known running joke rather than a precise record of each visit.
- **"2022 … करीब 60 प्रतिशत Denmark को, 40 प्रतिशत Canada को":** the 14 June 2022 agreement split the island along a natural ridge, giving Greenland (Kingdom of Denmark) about 60% and Canada about 40%. Denmark's parliament ratified it in December 2023.
- **"अपनी पहली ज़मीनी सरहद":** the split gave Canada and the Kingdom of Denmark a land border, their first.
