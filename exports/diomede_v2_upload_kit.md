## Fact notes

- "4 किलोमीटर" is the distance between the two islands (3.8 km); the mainlands are about 88 km apart. If a commenter says "88 km", that's the mainland figure.
- Diomede village: 83 people in the 2020 census.
- Time difference: 21 hours in winter, 20 in summer (Alaska daylight saving). The video shows US Sunday 3 PM = Russia Monday 12 noon.
- Crossing the ice between the islands isn't legal; the video says it's physically possible, not that it's allowed.
