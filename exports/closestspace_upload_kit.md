## Fact notes (checked 19 October 2026)

- **"Everest, 8,849 मीटर":** 8,848.86 m, the height Nepal and China announced together in December 2020.
- **"इसकी चोटी border पर है":** the summit lies on the border between Nepal and China (the Tibet Autonomous Region); the southern route is in Nepal, the northern in Tibet.
- **"हमारी धरती असल में गोल है ही नहीं":** the Earth is an oblate spheroid: its rotation makes it bulge at the equator and flatten at the poles. Its true shape, the geoid, is lumpier still because mass inside the Earth is unevenly spread.
- **"हज़ारों गुना बड़ा करके देखो":** the geoid's ups and downs are only about −105 m to +85 m against a 6,371 km Earth; the "potato" in the video is EGM96 exaggerated about 7,000 times.
- **"21 किलोमीटर ज़्यादा":** equatorial radius 6,378.1 km, polar radius 6,356.8 km: 21.4 km more at the equator.
- **"equator के पास … Chimborazo, 6,263 मीटर":** Chimborazo, an inactive volcano in Ecuador, is 6,263 m high and about 1.5° south of the equator.
- **"Everest से 2,600 मीटर छोटा":** 8,849 − 6,263 = 2,586 m.
- **"Everest से 2 किलोमीटर ज़्यादा दूर":** from the Earth's centre, Chimborazo's summit is about 6,384.4 km and Everest's about 6,382.3 km, about 2.1 km more.
- **"space के सबसे पास":** here this means farthest from the Earth's centre. Measured from sea level, Everest is still the highest mountain.
- **India's map:** where India is on screen it is shown in India's official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition); China's fill never takes Aksai Chin or Arunachal Pradesh.
