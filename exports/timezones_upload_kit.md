## Fact notes (checked 18 October 2026)

- **"24 zones":** the textbook figure for hourly zones; real offsets number about 38 (the :30 and :45 ones).
- **Greenwich, "0 वाली लकीर, UTC":** the prime meridian at Greenwich is the reference for UTC offsets.
- **"हमारा India है +5:30":** IST is UTC+5:30.
- **"IST जिस लकीर से गिना जाता है, वो Mirzapur से होकर गुज़रती है":** IST is calculated from 82.5°E, which passes the Mirzapur clock tower (within about 4 angular minutes; Wikipedia, Mirzapur Clock Tower). The line runs through the west of the city (Vindhyachal, part of Mirzapur-cum-Vindhyachal), so the video shows it splitting Vindhyachal from Mirzapur.
- **"सीधी लकीर होती, तो Mirzapur शहर ही बीच से कट जाता":** with straight hourly zones centred on multiples of 15°, UTC+5 runs to 82.5°E and UTC+6 starts there: a what-if.
- **France, "पूरे 12":** 12 time zones with its overseas territories, the most of any country (13 counting its Antarctic claim); Russia and the US have 11 each.
- **China, "चौड़ाई 5 time zones जितनी, पर घड़ी एक":** five zones were defined in the Republic era, never fully observed; Beijing Time (UTC+8) for the whole country since 1949.
- **Kashgar, "सर्दियों में सुबह 10 बजे तक सूरज नहीं निकलता":** a solar calculation (NOAA method) for 21 December gives sunrise at 10:12 Beijing time.
- **"लगभग 3,000 किलोमीटर चौड़ा":** India's official east–west extent is 2,933 km.
- **"गर्मियों में Arunachal में सूरज सुबह 4 बजे, Gujarat में 6 बजे के बाद":** a solar calculation for 21 June gives Dong 4:05 AM IST and Guhar Moti (Kutch) 6:11 AM IST.
- **"Assam के कई चाय बागानों में … बागान टाइम … 1 घंटा आगे":** tea-garden time is IST+1, introduced under British rule; still used informally in many gardens, not legally recognised.
- **Nepal, "15 मिनट आगे":** Nepal moved from IST to UTC+5:45 in 1986.
- **The close, "+6:30 ? प्रस्ताव":** a two-zone system for India has been proposed (CSIR-NPL, 2018, with the line at 89°52'E); shown as a proposal, not fact.
- **India's map:** the official boundary everywhere, including PoK, Gilgit-Baltistan, Aksai Chin and Arunachal Pradesh, in the country fill and in the time zone layer.
