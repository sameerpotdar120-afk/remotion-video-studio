## Fact notes

- **Five neighbours by land:** France, Portugal, Andorra, the United Kingdom (Gibraltar) and Morocco (at Ceuta, Melilla and the Peñón de Vélez de la Gomera).
- **Llívia:** a Spanish exclave surrounded by France, a short distance from the border (the video says about 1.5 km). The 1659 Treaty of the Pyrenees gave France the villages of the Cerdanya, and Llívia stayed Spanish because it counted as a town (vila), not a village.
- **Pheasant Island:** an island in the Bidasoa river under joint sovereignty, administered by Spain from February to July and by France from August to January. "इस वक्त फ्रांस का" is true only from August to January; this upload (October) is in France's half.
- **Andorra's two princes:** the co-princes are the President of France and the Bishop of Urgell (Spain).
- **Gibraltar:** British since the 1713 Treaty of Utrecht; it sits at the strait that opens the Mediterranean to the Atlantic.
- **The only European country with a land border with Africa:** Spain, through Ceuta and Melilla (and the Peñón de Vélez de la Gomera). Counted among sovereign European states.
- **85 metres:** the Peñón de Vélez de la Gomera was an island until a 1934 storm joined it to the coast with sand; its land border with Morocco, about 85 m, is often called the world's shortest.
