## Fact notes

- **"France से चार गुना से भी बड़ा":** the Tibetan plateau covers about 2.5 million km²; metropolitan France is about 0.55 million km², so roughly 4.5 times. The four France shapes on screen are drawn at true size for their latitude.
- **"दुनिया का सबसे ऊँचा पठार", "औसतन 4,500 मीटर":** the plateau's average elevation is usually given as over 4,500 m.
- **Mount Everest, 8,849 m:** the 2020 China–Nepal survey height. It sits on the plateau's southern edge, on the Nepal–China border.
- **"10,000 मीटर पर उड़ रहा है":** a typical airliner cruise height (about 33,000–39,000 ft).
- **"Masks बस कुछ ही मिनट":** cabin masks on most airliners run on chemical oxygen generators, which last about 12–15 minutes on common types (some up to about 22). The video says "कुछ ही मिनट" and gives no exact number.
- **"3,000 मीटर तक नीचे":** after a depressurisation, the emergency descent goes to about 10,000 ft (about 3,000 m), where people can breathe without extra oxygen.
- **"Airports गिनती के, पहाड़ों के बीच":** the plateau has only a handful of airports (Lhasa Gonggar, Shigatse, Nyingchi, Ngari Gunsa, Qamdo Bamda, Daocheng Yading and a few more), most above 3,500 m. The video gives no count.
- **L888:** a high-altitude route across western China over the plateau. Airlines need special approval and preparation to fly it: extra oxygen, escape-route ("drift-down") planning and suitable aircraft. That's why few airlines use it. The line on screen is approximate.
- **India:** shown with its official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition).
