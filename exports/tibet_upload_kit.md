## Fact notes

- **"25 लाख वर्ग किलोमीटर… भारत का लगभग तीन-चौथाई":** the Tibetan Plateau is about 2.5 million km²; India is 3.29 million km², so about 76%. The reference video's "5× France" is wrong: it is about 4.5×.
- **"औसतन 14,800 Feet":** the plateau's average elevation is about 4,500 m.
- **"35,000 Feet":** typical airliner cruise altitude (about 33,000–39,000 ft).
- **"Oxygen Masks… करीब 15 मिनट":** the chemical oxygen generators behind the masks last roughly 12–20 minutes, depending on the aircraft. "करीब" covers that.
- **"10,000 Feet तक नीचे":** the standard emergency-descent target after a depressurisation.
- **"ज़्यादातर Airlines":** deliberately not "all". Some routes and flights to Lhasa do cross the plateau.
- **"Airports गिने-चुने":** Tibet has only a handful of civil airports (Lhasa Gonggar, Shigatse, Nyingchi, Qamdo Bamda, Ngari Gunsa and a few more). The dots on screen mark those five. The reference's "only five" is softened to "गिने-चुने".
- **Map:** the plateau outline is the connected area above about 3,000 m from open elevation data. India is drawn with its official boundary.
