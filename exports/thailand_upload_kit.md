## Fact notes (checked 18 October 2026)

- **"Britain ने India पर लगभग 200 साल राज किया":** from Plassey (1757) to 1947 is 190 years (Company rule, then the Crown from 1858), hence "लगभग".
- **"कभी किसी का ग़ुलाम बना ही नहीं", "कभी किसी की colony नहीं बना":** Siam (Thailand) was never formally colonised, the only country in South-East Asia that wasn't. It did lose territory and signed unequal treaties, which the video shows.
- **"तीन जंगों के बाद … Burma … British India का हिस्सा":** three Anglo-Burmese wars (1824–26, 1852, 1885). Burma was governed as a province of British India until 1937.
- **"France … पहले Vietnam, फिर Cambodia":** Cochinchina from 1862, a protectorate over Cambodia from 1863, then Annam and Tonkin.
- **"एक झटके में निगल सकता था", "जंग लगभग तय थी":** figures of speech for the military gap and the risk.
- **"1896 … Chao Phraya नदी की घाटी को कोई नहीं छुएगा":** the January 1896 Anglo-French Declaration. Neither power would send armed forces into the Menam (Chao Phraya) basin without the other's consent.
- **"बीच का हिस्सा safe था, पर किनारे?":** the 1904 declaration set British influence west of the basin and French influence east. The land Siam later gave up was on its edges.
- **"French जंगी जहाज़ Bangkok तक … Laos":** the 1893 Franco-Siamese crisis. French gunboats forced their way past Paknam up the Chao Phraya and blockaded Bangkok. Siam ceded the land east of the Mekong; more of Laos went in 1904.
- **"Cambodia के कुछ हिस्से France को":** the 1907 treaty: Battambang, Siem Reap and Sisophon.
- **"Malaya के चार राज्य Britain को":** the 1909 Anglo-Siamese Treaty: Kedah, Kelantan, Terengganu and Perlis.
- **"राजाओं ने देश को तेज़ी से modern बनाया":** Kings Mongkut (Rama IV) and Chulalongkorn (Rama V) modernised the administration, army, railways and laws.
- **The skit:** a joke written for this video, not a quote from real diplomats.
- **Flags:** Siam is shown with its 1855–1916 flag (a white elephant on red); Thailand's tricolour came in 1917, and the name changed from Siam to Thailand in 1939.
- **India's map:** British India is shown in India's official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition) in every layer, including PoK, Gilgit-Baltistan and Aksai Chin.
