## Fact notes (checked 14 November 2026)

- **"करीब 200 साल राज":** Company rule from 1757 (Plassey) and Crown rule 1858-1947, about 190 years; "करीब 200 साल" is the
  usual phrasing.
- **"एक King, एक Passport, एक Army… चार देश… अपनी Capital":** one monarch, one British passport, one British Armed Forces;
  England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland; London, Edinburgh, Cardiff, Belfast.
- **"Cricket में England की team… England और Wales":** the team is run by the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB).
  **Football:** the four are separate FIFA members. **Olympics:** one team, Team GB.
- **"13वीं सदी में England ने Wales को जीत लिया… किलों के घेरे":** Edward I's conquest (1277-1283) and his castles
  (Conwy, Caernarfon, Harlech, Beaumaris and others). Legal annexation came later (Laws in Wales Acts 1535-1542).
- **"1603 में Scotland का राजा England की गद्दी का भी वारिस":** the Union of the Crowns: James VI of Scotland became James I
  of England and moved to London.
- **"Panama में colony… पैसा डूब गया… 1707":** the Darien scheme (1698-1700) lost a large share of Scotland's capital; the
  1707 Acts of Union kept Scots law, courts and the Church of Scotland. Great Britain was formed on 1 May 1707.
- **"1801 में वो Parliament ख़िताबों और पैसों से ख़रीदी गई":** the Act of Union 1800 (in force 1 January 1801) passed the
  Irish Parliament after peerages, offices and about £1.26 million in compensation to borough owners; widely described as
  bribery.
- **"1916 का Easter Rising… 1922":** Easter Rising 1916; War of Independence 1919-1921; Irish Free State 1922; Northern
  Ireland opted out (December 1922).
- **"Ireland का बँटवारा London की Parliament के क़ानून से… 1947 में भारत का भी":** Government of Ireland Act 1920 (Northern
  Ireland created 1921); Indian Independence Act 1947. Both are acts of the UK Parliament.
- **"United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland":** the official name since 1927.
