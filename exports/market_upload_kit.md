## Fact notes

- "पाँच फुटबॉल मैदान से भी छोटा": Märket is about 3.3 hectares. A standard pitch is about 0.7 ha, so five pitches are about 3.6 ha.
- "1809 … स्वीडन रूस से जंग हार गया": the Finnish War of 1808–09 ended with the Treaty of Fredrikshamn (September 1809). Finland became an autonomous Grand Duchy inside the Russian Empire.
- "बॉर्डर … सीधा इस टापू के बीच से": the boundary through the Åland Sea was drawn as a straight line that split Märket between Sweden and the Grand Duchy (whose border later passed to independent Finland, in 1917).
- "1885 … लाइटहाउस": built in 1885 by the Grand Duchy of Finland's maritime authorities. It ended up on the Swedish part of the island.
- "1985 … बॉर्डर ही मोड़ दिया": the two countries agreed a new line in 1985 that puts the lighthouse in Finland. Each side keeps the same land area as before, which is why the line zig-zags.
- The on-screen island shape and zig-zag are illustrative, not survey-accurate.
