## Fact notes (checked October 2026)

- **"60 KM लंबी दीवार", Bhavnagar to Bharuch:** the planned dam is 60.13 km long, of which 26.7 km is in the sea, from Kardej village (Bhavnagar district) to Paniyadra village (Bharuch district) (Wikipedia, citing the 2022 pre-feasibility report). The line on screen follows it only roughly.
- **"Mahi, Sabarmati और Dhadhar जैसी नदियाँ":** the reservoir is to take runoff from the Mahi, Sabarmati, Dhadhar and Saurashtra's coastal rivers. The revised alignment keeps the Narmada mouth outside the reservoir.
- **"हज़ारों करोड़ लीटर… हर साल":** no exact annual figure is claimed. For scale, the planned reservoir capacity is about 7,807 million cubic metres (about 7.8 lakh crore litres).
- **"भरती… Gates बंद, उतरेगी तो Gates खुलेंगे":** the creator's simplified description of how the tidal gates would let salt water out and keep river water in.
- **"1,600 वर्ग KM की मीठे पानी की झील":** the reservoir's planned surface area is about 1,600 km². It would be larger than any natural freshwater lake in India, hence "भारत की सबसे बड़ी मीठे पानी की झीलों में से एक".
- **"Saurashtra के 9 ज़िलों":** about 10 lakh hectares of irrigation across 42 talukas in nine Saurashtra districts. The 9 dots on screen are illustrative.
- **"16 Lane का Highway और Railway":** a 16-lane road and a four-lane railway on the dam top in the 2026 plan.
- **"356 KM से 177 KM":** the figure given in July 2026 news reports for Bhavnagar to Surat. Wikipedia's account of the 2026 filing gives different figures (about 240 km to about 60 km, Bhavnagar to Surat or Mumbai), so the video says "लगभग आधा".
- **"ज्वार 10 मीटर से ऊपर":** studies give a tidal range of about 8–11 m in the gulf.
- **"50 साल":** a UNDP expert identified the gulf for the project in 1975; feasibility work followed from the late 1980s. Main dam construction had not started as of 2026.
- **"₹1,33,000 करोड़":** the May 2026 environmental-clearance filing puts the cost at ₹1,33,246 crore (earlier estimates: ₹55,000 crore in the old plan, ₹1,00,200 crore in 2022). The 2026 filing projects construction from May 2028 and completion by late 2035.
- **"काल्पनिक सरोवर" / "कल्पना":** the creator's own wordplay on the name, not a quote.
- **India:** shown with its official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition).
