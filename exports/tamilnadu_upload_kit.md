## Fact notes (checked 26 Nov 2026)

- Tamil University, Thanjavur: established and inaugurated by CM M. G. Ramachandran on 15 Sep 1981 (Covai Post 2016;
  Wikipedia). Campus "900 acres" (Covai Post, Wikipedia; the university says about 850), "the largest university in the
  state by area" (Wikipedia).
- Five buildings shaped த மி ழ் நா டு spell தமிழ்நாடு around the library, within a 278 m radius (Covai Post). In
  OpenStreetMap the five footprints sit 274–306 m from the ring's centre, reading clockwise from the north. The library
  is not mapped in OSM, so the video marks its place with a ring and a label, not a drawn building.
- MGR died 24 Dec 1987. ழ completed 1989 ("two years after MGR's demise"); மி under the XI Plan; டு about 2015; த and
  நா by 2016 ("ends a wait of 35 years"). Single main source (Covai Post 2016).
- Uses in 2016: ழ Language faculty, மி Science, டு Developing Tamil (Covai Post, quoting the VC).
- ழ (zha) is a retroflex approximant; standard Hindi has no such sound. தமிழ் = Tamiḻ.
- Building heights in the 3D shots are slightly exaggerated so the rise reads from the air; footprints are real.
