What went wrong, in order of damage:

1. **The first 10 seconds (the biggest leak).** Nauru and Ireland lose half the audience by 10 s. The best reels keep 70 %
   or more. In both, frame 0 was a picture you have to decode (a satellite airport with an outline; a map of Ireland). The best
   one, the colour map, had a bold picture you read in a blink: 87 % still there at 3 s.
2. **Topics left India.** India stories hold (1947 averages 53 %, the colour map 55 %). Foreign ones bleed: Ireland 26 %,
   Nauru 33 %. Since Mumbai (our viral reel) the channel drifted to Ireland, Nauru, GTA, Six-Day, Korea and Baikonur.
3. **Length went up.** The five long reels (1:42 to 2:28) finish at 7–18 %. Reels are ranked on watch time relative to
   length, so a 1:50 reel that most people leave at 0:30 reads as a weak reel.
4. **The endings push people away.** GTA loses 14 points in its last 10 %, Nauru 9. The closing question plus the slow
   camera pull-back for the loop reads as "it's over". And the comment rate is 0.0 % on every reel, so those questions earn
   nothing.
5. **Steps in the middle.** Ram Setu and Andaman drop in steps at section changes: background sections with no payoff.

## 2. Platform and policy changes (what's real, what isn't)

- **Meta, India, 16–17 Nov 2026:** "An Update on the Content We Recommend on Instagram in India". The changes apply to
  recommendations **from accounts you don't follow** (Reels tab, Explore, Feed). Our reach is 76–86 % Reels tab, so this is
  exactly our traffic. The stated targets are sexually suggestive and violent content. Users can also switch on Political
  Content Control (fewer political posts from accounts they don't follow). No source says maps or geopolitics are targeted.
  (Sunday Guardian, Daily Pioneer, TNW/Reuters)
- **MeitY advisory, 8 Oct 2026** (reported as 10 Oct): platforms must "identify and appropriately label/contextualise
  manipulated, synthetically generated or misleading content" and take extra care with content that could affect public
  order. (MediaNama, full text)
- **IT (Amendment) Rules 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026:** AI-generated audio/visual content must be clearly labelled.
  Takedown deadline for unlawful content cut to 3 hours. (MeitY, DSCI, MediaNama)
- **Instagram 2026 ranking (third-party reports, not Instagram's own text):** watch time (including replays) and sends per
  reach matter most; unoriginal reposts lose recommendations.

Reading: the drop started before the mid-November change, so most of it is ours (section 1). The policy climate is a second
filter on top: anything that reads as political, disputed or "misleading" (borders in dispute, wars, communal history) now
carries more risk in non-follower reach. Science, places, people and money stories carry the least.

## 3. The next topic: "एवरेस्ट की चोटी, कभी भारत का समंदर थी"

**Why this one**
- In our niche right now, sea fossils high in the Himalaya are a proven breakout (Harshit Rautela, 13.8× channel median).
  Shekhar's "When India Was an Island!" did 10M.
- Known thing first (Everest, the Himalaya), a twist anyone gets in one second (sea creatures at 8,849 m), and an Indian twist
  on top: the summit rock was laid down on **India's** northern edge.
- A real Indian place with real people: Langza, Spiti (Himachal), where villagers find sea fossils at 4,400 m.
- Zero political or communal risk. 3D terrain is our strongest tool.
- Shareable: "अगली बार Spiti जाओ तो…".

Others weighed:
- "Half of India lives on a small part of the map" (density). The pattern is a proven English breakout (Geoff 130×) and
  nobody has done it in Hindi, but it needs a population dataset computed first. It's the next one.
- PoK/Aksai Chin (Geo Study 57×): skipped, disputed borders under the current policy climate.
- Notes on rupee notes: scrapped by the creator on 18 Nov.

## 4. How we tell it differently (beyond the hook score)

1. **Length 55–70 s.** Script about 150–170 Devanagari words. Our two best reels are 1:10 and 1:51, but the 1:51 one is a
   strong India story; for now aim short.
2. **Frame 0 is a bold picture anyone reads with the sound off:** Everest in 3D with sea water already over it and a fossil
   glowing on the summit. Three words at most.
3. **First payoff by 5–6 s,** not 8–10.
4. **One idea per 5–7 s; no background sections.** If a beat doesn't move the story, cut it.
5. **End hard on the strongest line,** with no closing question and no slow pull-back. The question goes in the pinned
   comment. The loop is a cut back to frame 0.
6. **A "send" moment,** a line viewers forward ("Spiti जाने वाले दोस्त को भेजो").
7. **Safe framing:** geography and science words in the title and caption. AI info on, and "AI illustration" on screen
   (IT Rules 2026). India's official boundary on every map.
8. **Measure:** after upload, the creator shares Instagram's 3 s / 10 s numbers. They go in the ledger in
   docs/HOOK_PLAYBOOK.md.
