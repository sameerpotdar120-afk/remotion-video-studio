## Fact notes

- "भारत के दो तिहाई": 2.14 million km² vs India 3.29 million km² = 65%.
- "तीन सेंट प्रति एकड़ से भी कम": $15M for about 530 million acres = 2.8 ¢ (the reference's "4 cents" is wrong).
- "लगभग दो गुना": the 1803 US was about 2.2 million km².
- "लाखों लोग": about 500,000 enslaved people in Saint-Domingue by 1790.
- "डिप्लोमैट्स को इजाज़त नहीं थी": Livingston and Monroe were authorized only for New Orleans (and the Floridas), up to $10M.
- "एक दिन में": the treaty was signed on one day (30 April 1803); "in a single day" follows the reference's framing.
