## Fact notes

- 64 km to Port Blair is the commonly cited figure (Wikipedia: "about 64 km west of Port Blair"). Measured centre-to-centre on the map it's about 56 km straight line, so the arrow shows the cited number.
- The no-go zone is 5 nautical miles (about 9.3 km) under the 1956 Protection of Aboriginal Tribes regulation.
- Population: never counted; most estimates are 50–200 (the on-screen "50–200?").
- Dec 2004: an islander was photographed aiming an arrow at an Indian Coast Guard helicopter after the tsunami.
