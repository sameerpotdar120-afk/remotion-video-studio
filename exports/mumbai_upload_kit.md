## Fact notes

- "दो करोड़ लोग": true for the Mumbai metropolitan region (about 2 crore+); the city proper was about 1.25 crore in the 2011 census.
- "1534": Treaty of Bassein, Gujarat Sultanate to Portugal.
- "1661 … दहेज": Catherine of Braganza's dowry on her marriage to Charles II (the transfer to the English happened in 1665).
- "10 पाउंड सालाना": Charles II leased the islands to the East India Company in 1668 for £10 a year.
- "1782 … सस्पेंड का ऑर्डर … 1784": Hornby started without the Company's approval; it sent a suspension notice, he carried on; finished 1784.
- "सातों द्वीप एक हो गए": the vellard closed the Great Breach; full merger of all seven took further reclamation, completed by about 1845.
- "एक सौ हेक्टेयर": the Coastal Road's reclaimed area is reported at about 100–111 ha.
