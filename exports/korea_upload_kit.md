## Fact notes (checked 9 November 2026)

- **"Korea की जंग 1953 में खत्म हुई ही नहीं":** the armistice of 27 July 1953 stopped the fighting; no peace treaty has been signed, so the two Koreas are technically still at war.
- **"73 साल":** 1953 to 2026.
- **"एक लाइन आज भी परिवारों को बाँटे हुए है":** families separated by the division; reunions have been rare and tightly limited.
- **"4 किलोमीटर की पट्टी":** the DMZ is about 4 km wide (2 km either side of the Military Demarcation Line) and about 248 km long, with fences, barbed wire and minefields.
- **"1945 तक एक ही देश, 35 साल Japan के कब्ज़े में":** Japan annexed Korea in August 1910 and ruled until its surrender in August 1945.
- **"उसी साल Soviet सेना उत्तर से घुस आई":** the USSR declared war on Japan on 8 August 1945 and its troops entered northern Korea from about 12 August. US troops landed only on 8 September 1945 (the reference video wrongly said they advanced from the south before the surrender).
- **"दो American officers… करीब आधे घंटे में… 38th parallel":** Dean Rusk and Charles Bonesteel chose the line on the night of 10-11 August 1945, working from a National Geographic map; Rusk later wrote that they had about 30 minutes. The USSR accepted it.
- **"बँटवारा कुछ समय के लिए था":** the line split the zones for taking Japan's surrender; the plan was a trusteeship before independence.
- **"1948 में दो देश":** Republic of Korea, 15 August 1948; DPRK, 9 September 1948. Both claim the whole peninsula.
- **"1950 में North ने हमला किया… लगभग पूरा South":** 25 June 1950; by September the South held only the Pusan (Busan) Perimeter.
- **"America और UN की फ़ौज… China की सीमा तक":** after the Incheon landing (15 September 1950), UN forces reached the Yalu in late October 1950.
- **"China कूदा, लाखों सैनिकों के साथ":** the "People's Volunteer Army" from October 1950. Figures vary (peak about 1.2-1.35 million at one time; Chinese sources give about 3 million in total by rotation), so the video says "लाखों".
- **"60 Para Field Ambulance… 627 लोग… 2.2 लाख से ज़्यादा मरीज़… 2,324 surgeries":** India sent no combat troops; this unit (Lt Col A. G. Rangaraj) served with the UN forces 1950-54. The numbers are from Indian Army and Ministry of Defence accounts (2,22,324 cases); there is no independent count.
- **"कम से कम 25 लाख मौतें":** estimates vary; most give 2.5 to 3 million soldiers and civilians.
- **"South Korea ने उस पर साइन तक नहीं किया":** the armistice was signed by the UN Command, North Korea and China; South Korea refused.
- **Front lines on the map:** approximate (38th parallel, Pusan Perimeter, near the Yalu, January 1951, the armistice line), as the on-screen credit says.
