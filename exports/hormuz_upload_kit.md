## Fact notes (checked 10-11 November 2026; the Hormuz situation changes weekly, so re-check on upload day)

- **"33 किलोमीटर का ये रास्ता" (VO) / "39 KM" (screen):** the strait is about **39 km (21 nautical miles)** wide at its
  narrowest, between the Quoin islands (Oman) and Larak (Iran). "33 km" is a common unit slip (21 miles read as statute
  miles). The screen shows the correct 39 KM; the VO line can be re-recorded and swapped in.
- **"रोज़ करीब 2 करोड़ बैरल… पाँचवाँ हिस्सा":** EIA, about 20 million b/d in 2024, about 20% of global petroleum liquids
  consumption.
- **"2 करोड़ 16 लाख से गिरकर, सिर्फ़ 49 लाख":** EIA figures, 21.6 million b/d (late 2025) to 4.9 million b/d (Q2 2026).
  EIA calls its tracking data since late February 2026 "especially unreliable"; other trackers are higher (IEA about 10.9
  million b/d in September). Traffic in early November 2026 was still far below normal.
- **"2026 की Iran जंग":** US-Israel strikes on Iran on 28 February 2026, and the conflict that followed.
- **"भारत का करीब आधा crude, रोज़ करीब 26 लाख बैरल":** Kpler put about 50% of India's crude imports through Hormuz in
  January-February 2026; India imported about 2.6 million b/d from the Gulf early in the year.
- **"Hajar पहाड़… करीब 700 km… करीब 3,000 m":** the range is about 700 km long; its highest peak, Jebel Shams (Oman), is
  about 3,009 m.
- **"Canal बना, तो दोनों देश बीच से कट जाएँगे":** a hypothetical canal across the base of Musandam (an Omani exclave) and
  Ras Al Khaimah (UAE) would split both. No such canal has been proposed seriously; the line on the map is illustrative.
- **"Saudi की East-West pipeline… Red Sea" / "UAE की pipeline… Fujairah":** Saudi East-West (Abqaiq to Yanbu), 5 million
  b/d (briefly 7 million in 2019); UAE Habshan-Fujairah, about 1.5-1.8 million b/d. Routes on the map are approximate.
- **"खाली जगह बस करीब 26 लाख बैरल रोज़":** EIA estimates about 2.6 million b/d of spare pipeline capacity could bypass
  Hormuz in a disruption.
- **"जितना अकेला भारत यहाँ से लेता था":** India's Gulf crude in early 2026 was about 2.6 million b/d, about the same as that
  spare capacity.
