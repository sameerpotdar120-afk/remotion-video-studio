## Fact notes (checked October 2026)

- **Togo's coastline, about 56 KM:** Togo has about 56 km of coastline on the Bight of Benin (Wikipedia, Geography of Togo; the CIA World Factbook comparison lists 56 km, against Benin's 121 km).
- **"Thailand को पलट दो… लगभग Myanmar":** a shape comparison, mirrored left to right, as in the reference. The shapes on screen are the real Natural Earth outlines.
- **Italy and Vietnam:** the boot is Italy's well-known shape; Vietnam's long S-shape is compared to it. Their areas are also close (Italy about 302,000 km², Vietnam about 331,000 km²), though the video doesn't say a number.
- **"दुनिया की दो सबसे लंबी मिर्ची":** the creator's playful line about Norway and Chile's long, thin shapes. Chile is often called the longest country north to south; Norway is not second, so the line is a joke, not a ranking.
- **Sicily as the football:** Sicily is the island off Italy's toe, shown as the ball, as in the reference.
- **India:** India does not appear with a drawn border in this video; the continent outlines in the hook use the India point-of-view edition.
