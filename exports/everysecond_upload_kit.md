## Fact notes (checked 11-12 November 2026)

- **"हर सेकंड… करीब 4 बच्चे… करीब 2 लोग":** UN WPP 2024: about 132.5 million births and 63.6 million deaths in 2026,
  about 4.2 and 2.0 a second.
- **"भारत में? करीब हर डेढ़ सेकंड में एक बच्चा":** about 23 million births a year in India (UN WPP 2024), one every 1.4 s.
- **"करीब 40 लाख emails":** about 350-380 billion emails a day (spam included), about 4 million a second.
- **"Google पर 1 लाख से ज़्यादा searches":** estimates of 8.5-14 billion searches a day, 100,000-160,000 a second.
- **"भारत अकेले करीब 9,500 UPI payments":** NPCI: 25.47 billion UPI transactions in October 2026, about 822 million a
  day, about 9,500 a second.
- **"करीब 44 बार बिजली… पुरानी किताबें 100 कहती थीं":** the 100/s figure dates from 1925; NASA's OTD/LIS satellites
  measure about 44 ± 5 flashes a second.
- **"करीब 1,200 बैरल तेल":** EIA, about 103-104 million barrels a day.
- **"करीब 1,200 टन CO2":** about 37-38 billion tonnes of fossil CO2 a year (Global Carbon Project), about 1,200 a second.
- **"रोशनी धरती के साढ़े 7 चक्कर":** light travels about 299,792 km a second; Earth's circumference is about 40,075 km.
- **"करीब 30 किलोमीटर आगे":** Earth's mean orbital speed is about 29.8 km/s.
