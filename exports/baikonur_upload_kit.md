## Fact notes

See `public/baikonur/script_draft.md` (checked 20 November 2026, with sources):
- Rakesh Sharma flew on Soyuz T-11 from Baikonur, 3 April 1984.
- Baikonur was founded in 1955 and is the world's first spaceport.
- Sputnik flew in 1957 and Gagarin in 1961.
- The USSR collapsed in 1991.
- Russia pays $115 million a year for the lease; set in 1994 and extended in 2004 to 2050.
- The lease area is about 6,717 km².
- Russian law applies in the city, and the rouble is mostly used.
- Latitudes: Baikonur about 46° N, Plesetsk 63° N, Vostochny 52° N.
- The Earth's surface speed is about 1,670 km/h at the equator, about 1,170 at Baikonur and about 760 at Plesetsk.
