## Fact notes (checked October 2026)

- **"Plan था 12 साल का":** he originally expected to finish the trek in about 12 years (Wikipedia).
- **"1998 में Chile से":** he set off from Punta Arenas, Chile, on 1 November 1998.
- **"Panama में 18 दिन जेल":** he was jailed for 18 days in Metetí for entering without a visa (Wikipedia). The reference video's on-screen "16 days" doesn't match its own voiceover; 18 is the verified figure.
- **"Bering Strait पर 14 दिन बर्फ़ पर":** he crossed on foot in March 2006 with Dimitri Kieffer, on a 14-day route over drifting ice, and was detained by Russian border guards near Uelen.
- **"90 दिन का Visa… Ban":** Russian visas limited him to 90 days at a time. Russia banned him in 2013, and the ban was overturned in October 2014. The script doesn't give the ban's length, because it was cut short.
- **"अकेले Russia में 11 साल":** the Russian section ran from 2006 until he crossed into Mongolia in 2017.
- **"Caspian Sea: 31 दिन में 288 km":** he swam it in August 2024, with Angela Maxwell and two Azerbaijani swimmers, resting on a support boat (Wikipedia).
- **"English Channel":** he landed on Shakespeare Beach near Dover on 5 October 2026 after about 4 days of swimming and resting on support boats.
- **"करीब 50,000 km":** news reports give about 31,000 miles.
- **"घर बस कुछ सौ km दूर":** about 400 km were left when he landed in Dover. Reports expect him to reach Hull in November 2026; that's a projection, not a confirmed arrival.
- **Visuals:** Karl is a stylised AI illustration based on a reference photo, not a real photo. The photo itself doesn't appear in the video.
