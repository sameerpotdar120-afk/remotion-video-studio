## Fact notes (checked before publishing)

- 3.8 km between the islands: the standard figure (about 2.4 mi).
- Time difference: Big Diomede uses Kamchatka time (UTC+12), Little Diomede Alaska time (UTC−9 in winter, UTC−8 in summer), so 21 hours in winter and 20 in summer. The clock in the video shows +21 घंटे (the winter figure). If a commenter says "20 hours", both are right depending on the season.
- The 1867 Alaska treaty's boundary runs midway between the two islands.
- Big Diomede has no civilian population today (its residents were moved to the mainland in 1948); only Russian border troops are stationed there.
- In winter the strait often freezes into an ice bridge. Crossing it is not allowed.
