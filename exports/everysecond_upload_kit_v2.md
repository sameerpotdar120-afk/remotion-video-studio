## Fact notes

Same sources and checks as yesterday's kit (`everysecond_upload_kit.md`, "Fact notes"). Two lines are new here:
- **"इस video को देखने में… 62 सेकंड… कितने बच्चे" (answer about 44):** India has about 23 million births a year (UN WPP
  2024), one every 1.37 s; 62.4 s ÷ 1.37 ≈ 45, so "about 44" is safe.
- **"जितनी देर में… 4 बच्चे… 9,500 UPI… 30 km":** the per-second figures from the video (UN WPP 2024; NPCI October 2026;
  Earth's orbital speed about 29.8 km/s).
