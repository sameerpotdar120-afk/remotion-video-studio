# Map sizes: image prompts

Only four images: everything else (the maps, Greenland/India/Africa fills, km lines, the globe, the six gores, the stretch, the parchment map, the ship and the straight-line route, the schoolbook) is drawn in code from Natural Earth.

Style for every image: photoreal studio cut-out, soft even light, sharp detail, natural colours, 4K, plain pure white #FFFFFF background, no shadow on the ground, no text, no logos, no watermark. The subject is BIG and fills 85–90 % of the frame, nothing cut off at the edges.
