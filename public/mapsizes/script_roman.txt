Kisi ne ye kabhi nahi bataya: India asal me Greenland se dedh guna bada hai. Fir ye chhota kyu dikhta hai?

Greenland ko upar se neeche naapo: 2,670 kilometer. Ab India ko naapo: 3,214 kilometer.

Ab poorab se pashchim. Greenland: 1,050 kilometer. India: 2,933.

Dono taraf India aage. Area me bhi: Greenland kareeb 22 lakh varg kilometer, India kareeb 33 lakh.

Par India ko uthakar Greenland par rakho, to ye uske saamne chhota sa lagta hai.

Aur Africa? Ye to Greenland se 14 guna bada hai. Fir bhi nakshe par dono lagbhag barabar.

To gadbad kahan hai?

1569 me Gerardus Mercator naam ke ek naksha banane wale ne ye map banaya, samundar ke naavikon ke liye. Is par do bandargahon ke beech ek seedhi line kheencho, compass usi disha par rakho, aur jahaaz pahunch jayega.

Isiliye ye map har jagah chha gaya. School ki kitaabon se lekar Google Maps ke app tak.

Par dikkat ye hai: gol dharti ko bina kheenche flat karna namumkin hai.

Dharti ko chhe tukdon me kaato. Unhe flat bichhao. Aur beech ki khaali jagah bharne ke liye unhe kheencho.

Jitna pole ke paas, utna zyada kheenchaav. Greenland ke beech me area kareeb 10 guna phool jaata hai. India ke paas bas kareeb 15%.

Ab Greenland ko uski asli size me India ke paas laao.

Ye hai asli Greenland. India se chhota. Aur ye baat...
