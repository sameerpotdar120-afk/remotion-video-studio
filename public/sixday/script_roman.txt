Tum Gaza, Golan aur West Bank ka naam roz news mein sunte ho. Par ye teeno naam sirf 6 din ki ek jung ne naqshe par likhe the. June 1967. Ek taraf Israel. Doosri taraf Egypt, Jordan aur Syria. Aur is jung ka faisla pehle din ke pehle 3 ghante mein hi ho gaya tha. Aaj, naqshe par, din-ba-din, poori kahani.

[0:30 · CHAPTER 1: NAQSHA]
Pehle naqsha samjho. 1967 se pehle Israel ek patli si zameen thi. Uski sabse patli jagah par, samundar se Jordan ki seema tak, sirf kareeb 15 kilometer. Itni patli ki ek tank aadhe ghante mein paar kar le.
Neeche Egypt. Uske paas Sinai ka bada registan, aur Arab duniya ki sabse badi fauj. Poorab mein Jordan, jiske paas tha West Bank aur purana Jerusalem. Aur upar Syria, Golan ki oonchi pahadiyon par.
1948 aur 1956 ki ladaiyon ke baad koi asli shanti nahi hui thi. Seema par golabaari aam baat thi. Aur 1967 ki garmiyon tak, teeno taraf fauj jama ho rahi thi.

[1:20 · CHAPTER 2: MAY 1967]
May 1967 mein sab kuch tezi se badla. Shuruaat hui ek report se. Soviet Union ne Egypt ko bataya ki Israel, Syria ki seema par fauj jama kar raha hai. Baad mein ye report galat nikli. Par tab tak der ho chuki thi.
14 May se Egypt ki fauj Sinai mein utarne lagi. Cairo ki sadkon par tanks ki parade hui, taaki poori duniya dekhe.
Sinai aur Gaza mein 1957 se UN ki ek shanti sena tainaat thi, UNEF. Aur us sena mein Bharat ka ek bada dal tha.
18 May ko Egypt ke kehne par UN ne ye sena hatana shuru kar diya. Aur sena ke jaate hi, Red Sea ke kinaare ek chowki khaali ho gayi. Sharm el-Sheikh. Wahi chowki, jo ek bahut patli gali par nazar rakhti thi.

[SHORT 1 · "TIRAN" · START]
Red Sea ke kone mein ek patli si samundri gali hai, aur 1967 mein isi gali ne ek poori jung shuru kar di.
Ye hai Straits of Tiran. Ek taraf Egypt ka Sinai, doosri taraf Saudi Arabia.
Red Sea par Israel ka ek hi bandargah tha, Eilat. Aur Eilat se bahar samundar tak jaane ka sirf ek raasta: yahi gali. Asia aur Africa se aane wale jahaaz, tel ke tanker, sab isi se guzarte the.
Israel 1957 mein hi saaf keh chuka tha: ye gali band hui, to wo ise jung ka elaan maanega.
Phir 22 May 1967 ko Egypt ke raashtrapati Nasser ne elaan kiya: Israel ke jahaaz ab Tiran se nahi guzrenge.
Bas, ulti ginti shuru ho gayi. 30 May ko Jordan ne Egypt ke saath raksha samjhauta kiya. 4 June ko Israel ki sarkar ne jung ka faisla le liya.
Aur 5 June ki subah, sirf 2 hafte baad, poora Middle East jal utha.
[SHORT 1 · END]

[CHAPTER 3: INTEZAAR]
Agle do hafte Israel ke liye bahut lambe the. Uski fauj ka bada hissa reservists the. Aam log, jo dukaan, khet aur daftar chhod kar morche par aa gaye the. Desh ka kaam-kaaj lagbhag ruk gaya tha.
1 June ko Moshe Dayan raksha mantri bane. Aur har kisi ke saamne ek hi sawaal tha: intezaar karein, ya pehla waar karein?
4 June ki raat jawab mil chuka tha.

Aur ab wo subah, jisne poori jung ka faisla 3 ghante mein kar diya.

[SHORT 2 · "3 GHANTE" · START]
Ek jung jo 6 din chali, uska faisla pehle 3 ghante mein hi ho gaya tha.
5 June 1967, subah 7 bajkar 45 minute. Egypt ke airbase par subah ki gasht khatam ho chuki thi, aur zyadatar jahaaz zameen par khade the.
Tabhi Israel ke jet Mediterranean ke upar se aaye. Itne neeche udte hue, ki radar unhe pakad hi na sake.
Pehle runway tode gaye, taaki ek bhi jahaaz uth na paaye. Phir zameen par khade jahaaz, ek-ek karke.
Pehli lehar mein hi kareeb 200 Egyptian jahaaz zameen par hi khatam ho gaye. Phir doosri lehar aayi. Dopahar tak Egypt ki air force lagbhag khatam thi. Aur usi din Jordan aur Syria ki air force ko bhi bhaari nuksaan hua.
Israel ne is hamle ko naam diya tha: Operation Focus.
Aasmaan haath se gaya, to zameen par ladne wali fauj bina chhatri ke reh gayi. Agle 5 din ki kahani, asal mein, un 3 ghanton ka nateeja thi.
[SHORT 2 · END]

Par usi subah, Gaza mein ek aur kahani chal rahi thi. Aur wo kahani Bharat ki thi.

[SHORT 3 · "GAZA KE BHARATIYA JAWAN" · START]
Gaza mein 1967 mein Bharat ke 14 jawan shaheed hue, aur Bharat us jung mein lada hi nahi tha.
Ye jawan UN ki shanti sena, UNEF, ka hissa the. 1957 se Egypt aur Israel ki seema par, Bharat ki battalions baari-baari tainaat rahin. Neeli topi, aur kaam sirf ek: dono taraf ko ladne se rokna.
May 1967 mein Egypt ke kehne par UN ne sena hatana shuru kiya. Bharat ke kai jawan Gaza mein apne jahaaz ka intezaar kar rahe the. Ghar laut'ne ke liye.
Tabhi 5 June ko jung chhid gayi, aur wo do faujon ke beech phans gaye. Golabaari mein Bharat ke 14 jawan shaheed hue, aur kai ghayal. Brazil ka bhi ek sainik maara gaya.
Unmein Captain Vijay Sachar bhi the.
Unhone kisi ki taraf se jung nahi ladi thi. Wo shanti ki topi pehne hue the.
[SHORT 3 · END]

[CHAPTER 5: SINAI]
5 June ki subah hi Israel ke tank Sinai mein ghus gaye. Teen division, teen raaste.
Uttar mein, samundar ke kinaare-kinaare, Rafah aur El Arish ki taraf. Beech mein Abu Ageila ka morcha, jise Ariel Sharon ki division ne raat mein ghera aur toda. Aur dakshin mein, registan ke beech se.
Egypt ki fauj ke paas tank the, morche the. Par sar par aasmaan nahi tha.
6 June ko Cairo se hukm aaya: Sinai khaali karo. Hukm jaldbaazi mein aaya, aur peeche hatti fauj registan ke tang raaston par phans gayi. Mitla aur Giddi ke darron par, Israel ke tank pehle pahunch chuke the.
8 June tak Israel ki fauj Suez Canal ke kinaare khadi thi. Sinai, jo Israel se lagbhag 3 guna bada hai, sirf 4 din mein haath badal chuka tha.

[CHAPTER 6: JERUSALEM AUR WEST BANK]
Usi pehle din, 5 June ko, Jordan ki topen Jerusalem aur Tel Aviv ke aaspaas golabaari karne lagin. Jawab mein Israel ne West Bank ki taraf kooch kiya.
Jerusalem tab ek bata hua shehar tha. Naya shehar Israel ke paas. Aur purana shehar, Old City, apni deewaron ke andar, Jordan ke paas.
Do din ki sadak-sadak ladai ke baad, 7 June ko Israel ke paratroopers Old City mein daakhil ho gaye.
Usi din Jordan ki fauj Jordan nadi ke paar laut gayi. Aur poora West Bank, Jenin se Hebron tak, Israel ke haath mein aa gaya.
Is ek hafte ne jo Jerusalem banaya, uski bahas aaj bhi duniya ki sabse mushkil bahson mein se ek hai.

Ab bacha tha sirf ek morcha. Upar, Syria. Aur yahan ladai shuru hui jung ke paanchve din.

[SHORT 4 · "GOLAN" · START]
Golan Heights ke liye 1967 ki jung ka aakhri din lada gaya, kyunki jo upar baitha, wahi neeche ka maalik.
Golan ek ooncha, pathreela pathaar hai, Syria aur Israel ke beech. Neeche Galilee ki ghaati, aur Sea of Galilee, Israel ka sabse bada meetha paani.
1967 se pehle Syria upar tha, aur Israel ke gaon neeche. Seema par golabaari aam thi.
Aur isi pathaar se nikalti hain Jordan nadi ki kuch dharaayein.
9 June ko Israel ne hamla kiya. Seedhi chadhai, pathreeli zameen, aur upar bane Syria ke bunker.
Do din ki ladai ke baad, 10 June ko Golan Israel ke haath mein tha. Aur usi shaam ceasefire lag gaya.
Aaj bhi Golan Israel ke paas hai, aur Syria ise apna maanta hai.
Kyunki naqshe par kuch sau meter ki unchaai, kabhi-kabhi poori seema se zyada keemti hoti hai.
[SHORT 4 · END]

[SHORT 5 · "6 DIN KA NAQSHA" · START]
Israel ka naqsha sirf 6 din mein teen guna se bhi zyada bada ho gaya tha.
5 June ki subah, Israel ek patli si zameen. Sabse patli jagah sirf kareeb 15 kilometer.
10 June ki shaam tak: Egypt se Sinai aur Gaza. Jordan se West Bank aur purana Jerusalem. Syria se Golan.
Israel ke kabze ka ilaaka teen guna se bhi zyada ho chuka tha.
Keemat? Israel ke kareeb 800 se 1,000 sainik. Arab deshon ke, andazon ke mutabik, 15 se 20 hazaar.
Par ye naqsha hamesha waisa nahi raha. 1982 mein ek shanti samjhaute ke baad Sinai Egypt ko laut gaya. 2005 mein Israel Gaza se hat gaya.
Par West Bank aur Golan ka sawaal aaj bhi khula hai.
[SHORT 5 · END]

Par is jung ka sabse ajeeb nateeja kisi registan ya pahadi par nahi hua. Wo hua ek nahar mein.

[SHORT 6 · "YELLOW FLEET" · START]
Suez Canal mein 1967 mein 15 jahaaz phanse, aur wo 8 saal tak wahin khade rahe.
Jung shuru hui, to kai jahaaz nahar ke beech raaste mein the. Egypt ne nahar band kar di, aur dono sire jahaaz duba kar rok diye.
15 jahaaz andar phans gaye. 14 Great Bitter Lake mein, aur ek Lake Timsah mein. Ek kinaare Israel ki fauj, doosre kinaare Egypt ki. Beech mein ye jahaaz.
Mahine beete, phir saal. Crew ne milkar ek club bana liya, aur 1968 mein apne khud ke Olympics bhi khele.
Registan ki peeli ret jahaazon par jamti gayi, aur duniya ne unhe naam diya: Yellow Fleet.
Aur jab tak nahar band rahi, Bharat se Europe jaane wale jahaazon ko poore Africa ka chakkar lagana pada.
Nahar 8 saal baad, June 1975 mein khuli. Aur 15 mein se sirf 2 jahaaz apne engine se ghar laut paaye.
[SHORT 6 · END]

[CHAPTER 8: JUNG KE BAAD]
Jung khatam hui, par shanti nahi aayi.
22 November 1967 ko UN Security Council ne Resolution 242 paas kiya. Seedhi baat: jung mein li gayi zameen lautao, aur badle mein har desh ko surakshit seema ka haq do. Ise kehte hain "land for peace".
Par usse pehle, Sitambar 1967 mein, Khartoum mein Arab deshon ki baithak mein elaan ho chuka tha: Israel se na shanti, na manyata, na baatcheet. Ise "teen naa" kaha gaya.
Aur 6 saal baad, 1973 mein, Egypt aur Syria ne ek aur jung chhedi, Sinai aur Golan wapas lene ke liye.
Usi jung ke baad baatcheet shuru hui. 1979 mein Egypt aur Israel ke beech shanti samjhauta hua, aur 1982 tak poora Sinai Egypt ko laut gaya.

To ye thi wo jung, jo 6 din chali. Ek gali band hui, 3 ghante mein aasmaan ka faisla hua, aur 6 din mein ek naya naqsha bana.
