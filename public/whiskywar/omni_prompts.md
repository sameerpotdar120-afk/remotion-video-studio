**null_wk_B.mp4** (the middle, about 3.5 s: "अब Canada ने क्या किया होगा? जंग का ऐलान?")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He builds up a dramatic moment and then laughs it off:
0–3 s: he leans in a little and slowly raises both open hands to the sides of his face in mock alarm, as if saying "oh no".
3–6 s: he freezes for a beat, then wags one finger side to side, as if saying "no, no, no".
6–10 s: his shoulders bounce in a small friendly laugh and he waves one hand dismissively, then rests his hands near the microphone.

Smooth, playful, expressive movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
