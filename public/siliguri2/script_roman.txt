Tum Darjeeling jao ya Guwahati, tumhe sirf isi 22 kilometer ki patti se guzarna padta hai. Aur iske theek upar China ki fauj baithi hai.
Ye hai Siliguri Corridor, Bharat ka Chicken's Neck. Northeast ke 8 rajya, kareeb 4.5 crore log, sab isi se jude hain.
Upar, Sikkim aur Bhutan ke beech ghusi hui hai China ki Chumbi Valley. 2017 mein China yahin Doklam par sadak bana raha tha, us pahadi ki taraf jahan se poora Corridor dikhta hai. Bharat ki fauj 73 din tak saamne khadi rahi.
Ab neeche dekho. 2025 mein Bangladesh ne Lalmonirhat ka World War wala purana airbase phir se jagane ki baat ki, Bharat ki seema ke bilkul paas.
Aur Dhaka mein ek group ne "Greater Bangladesh" ka map dikhaya, jismein poora Northeast Bangladesh mein tha. Ye map Bharat ki Sansad tak pahuncha.
To Bharat ne is patli gardan ko kila bana diya. Hasimara mein Rafale, reports ke mutabiq S-400 air defence, aur Army ke 3 naye thikaane: Bamuni, Kishanganj aur Chopra.
Aur jab koi pooche ki ek patli si patti ke liye itni taiyari kyun, to bas itna kehna...
