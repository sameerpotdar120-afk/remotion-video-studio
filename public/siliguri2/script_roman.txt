Tum Darjeeling jao ya Guwahati, tumhe sirf isi 22 kilometer ki patti se guzarna padta hai. Aur iske theek upar baithi hai China ki Army.

Ye hai Siliguri Corridor, Bharat ka Chicken's Neck. Northeast ke 8 Rajya, kareeb 4.5 crore log, sab isi se jude hain.

Upar, Sikkim aur Bhutan ke beech ghusi hui hai China ki Chumbi Valley. 2017 me China yahin Doklam par sadak bana raha tha, us pahaadi ki taraf jahan se poora Corridor dikhta hai. Bharat ki Army 73 din tak saamne khadi rahi, aur sadak wahin ruk gayi.

Ab neeche dekho. Bharat ke Border ke bilkul paas, Bangladesh ka Lalmonirhat, World War ke zamaane ka ek purana Airbase. 2025 me Bangladesh ne ise phir se jagane ki baat ki.

Aur Dhaka me ek group ne Greater Bangladesh ka Map dikhaya, jisme poora Northeast Bangladesh me tha. Ye Map Bharat ki Parliament tak pahuncha.

To Bharat ne is patli Gardan ko Kila bana diya. Hasimara me Rafale Fighter Jets, Reports ke mutabik S-400 Air Defence, aur Army ke 3 naye Garrison: Bamuni, Kishanganj aur Chopra.

Aur jab koi pooche ki ek patli si patti ke liye itni taiyaari kyun, to bas itna kehna...
