# Madeira: reference breakdown

Reference: "This Island Burned for 7 Years - The Secret History of Madeira" (vertical, 720×1280, 25 fps, 62.0 s). English VO, about 150 words, roughly 145 wpm. A music bed runs the whole way, with a hit effect on almost every cut or pop (dense transients in the spectrum).

## Look

- Satellite map, dark navy ocean with faint seabed relief, land kept natural (greens, browns, Sahara sand). It opens bright on Europe and Africa, and the grade drops darker as the camera dives toward the Atlantic.
- Highlights are **red neon outlines** (thin bright red stroke plus an outer red glow) that pulse. Fills strobe: a yellow-green fill flashes on and off with the red outline, three times, about 4 frames per state.
- Labels are white bold sans caps with a deep red glow ("MADEIRA", "$8 BILLION ECONOMY"). Each letter drops in from above and scales from small, about 2 frames per letter, landing with a small bounce.
- 3D props sit on the map in a light oblique view: pine forest, caravel, sugar cane clumps, wine bottle (a real product shot), real Ronaldo photo (we can't use either).
- No captions on screen.

## What we keep, what we replace

- Keep: the dive from Europe, the red neon outlines with strobe, the letter-drop red-glow title, the forest that grows, the fuse and the spreading fire, the caravel voyage with the locked camera, sugar cane popping and sinking, the flipping photo cards, the zoom-through prop, the outline draw with a big number, the zoom-out end.
- Replace: Google-style imagery with our EOX Sentinel-2 plates (Twins look, flat top-down, no 3D tilt per the house map rule); the real wine bottle with our own unbranded UE5 bottle; the stock drone footage with our own GPT photo cards; the real Ronaldo photo with a fictional footballer seen from behind in a white number-7 kit (no likeness), plus the airport's name as a label.
- Our loop ending replaces the reference's end card.
