# Hormuz: image prompts

Only three images. Everything else is built in Blender or drawn in code: the 3D terrain (DEM + Sentinel-2), the raised
country slabs (Iran, UAE, Oman, Saudi Arabia, India with its official boundary), the strait gauge "33 KM", tanker lanes
and 3D oil drums, the canal, the Hajar range rising with its height counter, the two pipelines, the 26 lakh vs 2 crore
bars, flags, icon badges (security, customs, military) and all labels.

Style for every image: photoreal studio cut-out, soft even light, sharp detail, natural colours, 4K, plain pure white
#FFFFFF background, no shadow on the ground, no text, no logos, no watermark. The subject is BIG and fills 85–90 % of
the frame, nothing cut off at the edges.
