33 kilometer ka ye raasta. Aaj ye lagbhag band hai, iska asar tumhare petrol tak pahunchta hai, aur iske badle aaj tak koi doosra samudri raasta nahi bana. Kyu?

Ye hai Strait of Hormuz. Roz kareeb 2 crore barrel tel, yaani duniya ki khapat ka kareeb paanchva hissa, yahin se nikalta tha.

2026 ki Iran jung ke baad se, yahan jahaazon ka aana-jaana bahut kam ho gaya hai. Roz 2 crore 16 lakh barrel se girkar, sirf 49 lakh.

Aur Bharat? Is saal ki shuruaat me hamara kareeb aadha crude, yaani roz kareeb 26 lakh barrel, isi raaste se aata tha.

Map dekhkar lagta hai, bas yahan se ek canal kaat do. Persian Gulf se seedhe Gulf of Oman.

Par raaste me khade hain Hajar pahaad. Kareeb 700 kilometer lambi range, aur sabse oonchi choti kareeb 3,000 meter.

Upar se ye zameen do deshon ki hai: UAE aur Oman.

Canal bana, to dono desh beech se kat jaayenge. Security, customs, fauj, sab do hisson me.

To Gulf ne kiya kya? Canal nahi, pipelines.

Saudi ki East-West pipeline tel ko Red Sea tak le jaati hai, aur UAE ki pipeline seedhe Fujairah tak, Hormuz ke bahar.

Par Hormuz band ho, to in pipelines me khaali jagah bas kareeb 26 lakh barrel roz ki hai. Yaani utna hi, jitna akela Bharat yahan se leta tha. 2 crore ke saamne, bahut kam.
