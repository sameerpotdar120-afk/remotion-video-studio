# Panama Canal: Omni prompts for Null

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** the file name above each clip (2–3 takes each, e.g. `null_pc_B1.mp4`, `null_pc_B2.mp4`).

This video ends in a seamless loop, so Null appears only in the middle (no clip at the end).

```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He explains a balance between two things:
0–3 s: he raises one finger as if saying "wait, there's more", then lowers it.
3–6 s: he mimes lifting a glass of water to his face with one hand, then lowers it.
6–10 s: he holds both palms up side by side like the two pans of a weighing scale and gently moves them up and down in turn, then rests his hands.

Smooth, calm, explanatory movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
