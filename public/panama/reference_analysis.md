# Reference analysis: "A Ship Paid $5.3 Million to Skip the Panama Canal Line"

1080×1920, 30 fps, 66.6 s (1,998 frames), English VO at about 2.9 words/s, karaoke captions small and low
(y ≈ 1330, white with the spoken word in yellow). Analysed from contact sheets every 0.5 s and micro-frame strips
(every 3–4 frames) through each move. Times are the reference's seconds.

## Look

- **Map:** a top-down satellite map (Google-Earth-like imagery, which we can't use) with a strong teal grade: turquoise
  shallow sea, saturated green jungle, warm haze on the coast. Slight tilt and soft depth-of-field blur on the far edge
  (tilt-shift feel). We rebuild it from EOX Sentinel-2 + terrarium relief + our GeoLayers grade.
- **Labels:** small dark rounded boxes with white text ("Panama Canal", "Atlantic", "Pacific", "Gatun Lake",
  "Waiting", "Canal: 51 mi") with a thin leader to the point; they pop with a tiny overshoot.
- **Big numbers:** white, bold, glowing, counting up (odometer-style random digits) and settling: "$5,300,000",
  "52,000,000 gal", "27 → 36 ships a day", "$1,773,513 → $5,300,000".
- **Title cards:** red stamped sticker "RUNNING DRY" under white "PANAMA CANAL" (frame 0); yellow pill "$5.3M SKIP
  THE LINE"; red stamp "TRAFFIC CUT AGAIN"; calendar tile "SEPTEMBER 2026"; dark pill "Refills only with rain",
  "Would You Pay $5.3M?".
- **Two 2D scenes:** a flat vector cross-section of the locks (sky blue, green hills, brown earth, grey lock walls,
  blue water, a tiny container ship), and a navy "pools" infographic.
- **Character:** a chibi Panamanian man (big round head, white shirt with a Panama flag badge) on photo-style
  backgrounds (dried cracked lakebed with stranded ships; Panama City seafront with a drinking fountain). Expressive
  face swaps: crying, shocked "!", confused "?".
- **Transitions:** whip-zooms and whip-pans with heavy motion blur (map → map), a vertical blur-slide wipe
  (infographic → map, 29.1 s), a blur dissolve (map → photo scene, 34.5 s), a swish zoom-out to the continent
  (59.0 s) and a fast fly-back to the queue (62.0 s).

## Sound

- **Music:** a light, low, steady electronic bed under the whole video (no big drops), ducked under the voice.
- **SFX (from the audio onsets):**
  - soft "pop"/click on every label and tag (≈ 0.2, 3.4, 5.9, 7.1, 9.1, 10.4, 11.2, 12.2 s …)
  - odometer ticking under every counter (1.0–2.0, 22–25, 30–31, 38–39, 44.5–46 s)
  - whooshes on every whip zoom and wipe (4.1, 12.0, 26.0, 29.1, 34.5, 42.2, 50.4, 59.0, 62.0 s)
  - water: rising water in the locks (12–14 s), a long pour along the arc (21–25 s)
  - pool tiles: rapid tiny blips (26.2–28 s)
  - a sad "womp" and tear drops (37–38 s), a "!" sting (38.4 s)
  - a stamp thud on "TRAFFIC CUT AGAIN" (49.1 s)
  - a heartbeat monitor beep (56–58 s) and a flatline tone (58.1 s)
  - a final whoosh into the loop.
- Bass hits under the big numbers (5.3 M at 2.0 s and 45.4 s, 52 M at 23.9 s, 2023 at 34.9 s).

## Rhythm

A cut, label or counter every 1.0–2.5 s; the camera never stops (slow push or drift between hits). Numbers land on
the stressed word. The loop ending matches frame 0 exactly.
