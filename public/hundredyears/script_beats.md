## Fact notes (checked 19 October 2026)

- **"116 साल", "1337", "1453":** the war ran from Philip VI's confiscation of Gascony (1337) to Castillon (1453). It was on and off, with long truces.
- **"माँ France की राजकुमारी":** Edward III's mother Isabella was the daughter of Philip IV of France. Edward claimed the French crown through her.
- **"17 साल की किसान की बेटी":** Joan was born about 1412 at Domrémy, the daughter of a farmer (Jacques d'Arc). She was about 17 in 1429.
- **"longbow से पंगा":** English longbowmen were decisive against the French cavalry at Crécy (1346) and Agincourt (1415).
- **"राजा कमज़ोर, सरदार आपस में":** Charles VI's madness and the Armagnac–Burgundian civil war.
- **"ऊपर से आवाज़":** Joan said she heard the voices of Saints Michael, Catherine and Margaret.
- **Orléans:** the siege was lifted in May 1429, and Joan was wounded by an arrow at Les Tourelles.
- **Reims:** after the Loire campaign (June 1429, Patay), Charles VII was crowned at Reims on 17 July 1429.
- **"Burgundy के सैनिक … पैसों के बदले England को":** she was captured at Compiègne (May 1430) and handed to the English for 10,000 livres.
- **Rouen:** a church trial under Bishop Cauchon. She was burned on 30 May 1431, aged about 19. The verdict was overturned in 1456 and she was made a saint in 1920.
- **"1453 में France ने ये जंग जीत ली":** England kept only Calais, until 1558.
- **Maps and flags:** England means England and Wales (Scotland was France's ally). England flies St George and the royal leopards, France the fleur-de-lis. No Union Jack, no tricolour.
