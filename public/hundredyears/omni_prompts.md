**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** the file name above each clip (2–3 takes each, e.g. `null_hy_A1.mp4`, `null_hy_A2.mp4`).

**null_hy_A.mp4** (the end, about 8 s: "Church ने जिसे चुड़ैल कहकर जलवाया था, लगभग 500 साल बाद उसी Church ने उसे Saint बना दिया। ये इंसाफ़ था, या धोखा? Comment में लिखो।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He finishes a story and puts a question to the audience:
0–3 s: he holds both hands out, palms up, at the same height, then slowly raises one and lowers the other, like a balance weighing two sides.
3–6 s: he tilts his head a little and turns his palms toward the camera, as if asking "which one?".
6–10 s: he points at the camera with one hand, then makes a small typing motion with his fingers near the microphone, and rests his hands.

Smooth, friendly, explanatory movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
