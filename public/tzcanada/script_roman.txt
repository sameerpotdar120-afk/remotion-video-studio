Agar tum Vancouver se Seattle ki train lo, to schedule ke hisaab se jaane me 3 ghante lagenge, aur waapsi me poore 5.

Kyu? Pehle America aur Canada ka time zone map dekho. Dekhne me bilkul normal hai. Poorab ya pashchim jao, to ghadi ek ghanta aage ya peeche.

Par 1 November ki raat 2 baje, sab ulta-pulta ho gaya.

Alberta ke Calgary aur Florida ke Destin ko dekho. Dono ke beech 3,000 kilometer se zyada ki doori hai, aur karib 28 degree longitude ka fark. Phir bhi ab dono ka time ek hi hai.

Aur yahan ulta hua. Vancouver aur Seattle lagbhag ek hi longitude par hain, fark ek degree se bhi kam. Par dono ki ghadiyon me poora ek ghante ka fark.

Isiliye train ka safar dono taraf lagbhag 4 ghante ka hi hai, par ghadi dekho to jaana 3 ghante ka lagta hai, aur aana 5 ghante ka.

Aise aur bhi kai ajeeb maamle ab poore nakshe par hain. Par ye hua kaise?

British Columbia, Alberta, Manitoba aur Northwest Territories ne is saal ghadiyan peeche karna hi chhod diya. Saskatchewan aur Yukon to pehle se aisa karte aa rahe hain.

To jab baaki sab ne apni ghadiyan ek ghanta peeche kin, ye wahin ruke rahe, aur poora naksha khisak gaya.

March me baaki ghadiyan phir aage hongi, to naksha phir seedha dikhega. Par tab tak...
