# Malacca: Omni prompts for Null (two short clips)

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach
`public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** the file name above each clip (2 takes each if you can, e.g.
`null_ml_A1.mp4`, `null_ml_A2.mp4`).

Null appears in the middle only, in split screen, about 3–4 s each, about 7 s in total. Never at the start or the end
(the video loops).

**null_ml_A.mp4** (the line "China me is darr ka naam hi pad gaya: Malacca Dilemma.")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He shows something being squeezed through a tiny gap:
0–3 s: he holds both hands apart at shoulder width, palms facing each other.
3–6 s: he slowly presses his palms closer and closer together until they almost touch, as if squeezing through a narrow passage.
6–8 s: he gives a knowing half-nod and taps one finger on the desk twice.
8–10 s: he rests both hands calmly on the desk.

Clear, deliberate movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
