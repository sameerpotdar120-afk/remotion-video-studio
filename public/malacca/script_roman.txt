Tum jis Andaman-Nicobar mein chhuttiyan manate ho, uske theek neeche se China ka zyadatar tel guzarta hai. Aur yahi China ka sabse bada darr hai.

China ke paas duniya ki sabse badi Navy hai, 370 se zyada jahaaz. Bharat ke paas kareeb 140.

Par China ka kareeb 80% videshi tel, ek patli si gali se hokar aata hai: Malacca Strait.

Aur is gali ke darwaze par baitha hai Bharat ka Andaman-Nicobar. Indira Point se Indonesia sirf kareeb 145 kilometer.

Yahin hai Bharat ka ikloota Tri-Service Command. Army, Navy aur Air Force, ek saath.

China mein is darr ka naam hi pad gaya: "Malacca Dilemma".

Isliye China ne Bharat ke chaaron taraf Ports pakde: Pakistan ka Gwadar, Sri Lanka ka Hambantota, 99 saal ki lease par, Myanmar ka Kyaukpyu, aur Africa ke Djibouti mein uska pehla videshi military base. Ise kehte hain String of Pearls.

Bharat ka jawab: Iran ka Chabahar, Oman ka Duqm, aur Indonesia ka Sabang, jo Malacca ke bilkul muhaane par hai.

Aur 2035 tak Bharat ki Navy ka target hai, kareeb 200 jahaaz.

To agli baar Andaman-Nicobar jao, to yaad rakhna...
