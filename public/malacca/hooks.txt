Andaman, jahan tum chhuttiyan manane jaate ho, wahi China ki sabse badi kamzori ke theek saamne baitha hai.
China ke paas duniya ki sabse badi Navy hai, phir bhi wo ek samundar mein India se darta hai.
China ka kareeb 80% tel ek patli si gali se guzarta hai, aur us gali ke darwaze par Bharat baitha hai.
Tum Andaman ke beach par baithe ho, aur saamne se China ka zyadatar tel guzar raha hai.
Duniya ki sabse badi Navy ka sabse bada darr hai Andaman.
