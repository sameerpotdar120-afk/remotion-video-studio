# Thailand: Remotion motion pieces

Every character and moving prop in this video is drawn in code: no GPT parts, no copied art. The country characters use the real outlines from Natural Earth (public domain). All pieces are tested and committed in `src/thailand/motion/`, with their own entry point (`index.ts`, `Root.tsx`), 60 fps, text-free (captions and chips come from the main edit).

The main edit imports these components directly, so nothing has to be pre-rendered. The commands at the bottom are only for previews or for handing a piece to another editor.

## Shared parts

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Char.tsx` | **Country character rig**: Britain, France and Siam (1890s extent) as their real outlines with googly eyes, brows, six moods (happy, sad, angry, shifty, scared, smug), blinks on a seeded timer, a talking mouth, stick legs with shoes (walk, brace, shuffle), and arms that are sticks with white gloves or big flexing cartoon arms (two-bone IK). |
| `Chibi.tsx` | **Chibi rig**: a big round head, tube body and short legs, with five outfits. British diplomat (red coat, gold trim, dark hair). French diplomat (navy uniform, white pith helmet, medals, moustache). King of Siam (gold robe, tiered pointed crown, drawn with dignity). Redcoat (shako, cross-belts, musket). Burmese soldier (head wrap, spear). Poses are numbers (hand targets, stick angle, head turn and tilt, lean, squint, brows, crossed arms, hop) and blend smoothly. |
| `Flags.tsx` | Period flags drawn in code: the Union Flag, the French tricolour, and Siam's 1855–1916 white elephant on red. Plus `FlagBall`, the round flag pin with a rim, gloss and shadow. |
| `shapes.ts` | The three outlines as SVG paths (generated by `tools/thailand_char_shapes.py` from `src/thailand/geo.json`). |
| `theme.ts`, `Paper.tsx` | Colours (matching the map fills: Britain #B51CC9, France #F03A0B, Siam #F2D80A), easing helpers, and the paper backdrop for the white cutaways. |

## On the map (built in the main edit, not here)

These are map layers, done the same way as our previous videos (EOX Sentinel-2 plates, our grade, flat fills with sheen, edge draw-on and glow, boxed labels on pins, a restless camera), with India in its official boundary in every layer:

- British India filling purple. Burma recolouring teal → purple. France's red wiping down Vietnam, then Cambodia going ochre.
- The Thailand → Siam label crossfade.
- Push arrows into Burma, Laos, the Cambodian provinces and the Malay states, each with a thud.
- Sphere hatching (purple west, orange east) and the green Chao Phraya core.
- The gunboat's route up the river to Bangkok.
- The colonies greying out at the end.

## Render commands (previews only)

```bash
# one piece, 4K 60 fps, transparent (VP9 with alpha)
npx remotion render src/thailand/motion/index.ts t06-diplomats out/t06_diplomats.webm --scale=2 --codec=vp9 --pixel-format=yuva420p --image-format=png

# a piece with its paper backdrop, 4K 60 fps
npx remotion render src/thailand/motion/index.ts t01-tug-of-war out/t01_tug_of_war.mp4 --scale=2 --codec=h264 --crf=16

# the review reel, 1080×1920
npx remotion render src/thailand/motion/index.ts t99-reel out/thailand_motion_reel.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=18
```
