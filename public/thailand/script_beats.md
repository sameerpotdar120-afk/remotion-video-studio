## Fact notes (checked 18 October 2026)

- **"Britain ने India पर राज किया … ठीक बगल में … कभी किसी की colony नहीं बना":** Siam (Thailand) was never formally colonised, the only country in SE Asia that wasn't.
- **"तीन जंगों के बाद Burma … British India का ही हिस्सा":** three Anglo-Burmese wars (1824–26, 1852, 1885). Burma was governed as a province of British India until 1937.
- **"France … पहले Vietnam, फिर Cambodia":** Cochinchina from 1862, a protectorate over Cambodia from 1863, then Annam and Tonkin.
- **"सरहद मिलते ही जंग का ख़तरा":** the core of the buffer reading of the 1896 Anglo-French Declaration. Historians also credit Siamese diplomacy (Jeshurun, Journal of the Siam Society), which the ending reflects.
- **"1896 … Chao Phraya नदी की घाटी में कोई भी अपनी फ़ौज नहीं भेजेगा":** the January 1896 Declaration: neither power would advance its armed forces into the Menam (Chao Phraya) basin without the other's consent.
- **"बीच वाला हिस्सा कोई नहीं छुएगा… पर किनारों पर सबकी नज़र थी":** the 1904 declaration set British influence west of the Menam basin and French influence east, and the land Siam later gave up was on its edges.
- **"French जंगी जहाज़ Bangkok तक … Laos":** the 1893 Franco-Siamese crisis. French gunboats forced their way past Paknam up the Chao Phraya and blockaded Bangkok, and Siam ceded the land east of the Mekong (the rest of Laos went in 1904).
- **"Cambodia के कुछ इलाके France को":** the 1907 treaty: Battambang, Siem Reap and Sisophon.
- **"Malaya के चार राज्य Britain को":** the 1909 Anglo-Siamese Treaty: Kedah, Kelantan, Terengganu and Perlis.
- **"राजाओं ने देश को तेज़ी से नया बनाया":** Kings Mongkut (Rama IV) and Chulalongkorn (Rama V) modernised the administration, army, railways and laws.
- **The skit:** a joke written for this video, not a quote from real diplomats.
- **India:** British India is shown with India's official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition) in every layer.
