# UK: one country or four? Reference analysis, fact check, our angle

Reference: English Short, 83.4 s, 720×1280, **30 fps** (2,498 frames). Every frame of 0-6 s was checked one by one,
the rest at 2 fps; ffmpeg's scene detector finds **no hard cuts at all** (threshold 0.25): one continuous camera for 83 s.
The file only holds 30 real frames a second, so sampling at 120 fps would show each frame four times; per-frame (30 fps)
is the finest real view.

## Frame by frame: what it does

**0.00-0.50 s (frames 0-15): the kinetic-type open.** Frame 0 is a giant white serif "ing" (of "Kingdom") filling the
screen at an angle over the red UK on satellite. The camera pulls back fast: by frame 9 "Kingdom" is readable, by frame
15 "United Kingdom" sits as a curved label on the UK. The title is the picture: no still frame, no empty frame.
**0.5-1.5 s:** keeps pulling back to Europe; the UK (red fill) gets smaller, the label shrinks with it, captions write on
word by word ("Did you know" / "that the United Kingdom").
**1.5-2.8 s:** the camera settles; at "has one king" a yellow hexagon badge with a crown springs out of the UK (scale
overshoot over about 6 frames, glow ring).
**2.8-3.6 s:** "one passport": a blue hex badge springs out beside it. **3.6-4.3 s:** "one army": a red hex badge with a
soldier. The three badges sit as a cluster, each with a soft glow and a light bob.
**4.3-5.0 s:** "but inside it": the badges shrink into the UK as the camera dives in.
**5.0-6.0 s:** "there are four": the UK flashes white country by country (Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales, England, about
4 frames apart), then each white fill fades into its own colour (blue Scotland, green Wales, orange NI, red England).
The white flash is the transition device; it is used again for Great Britain and the full name later.

**Body (6-83 s), every beat a new visual:**
- Country names write on as they're said; each capital gets a pin and a label.
- Big "1" vs "1 2 3 4" numerals over the map for "one country or four?".
- History beats are told with **flags as characters**: the St George's cross with a crown sits on England; Wales gets a
  dragon flag; flags get **cartoon eyes** (Wales looks shocked, Ireland looks annoyed) and little swords.
- "XIII" (13th century) and "1603", "1801" in a large serif face.
- A ring of grey castle icons pops along the Welsh border one by one.
- A crown hops from Scotland to London; a sinking ship for Scotland's money trouble.
- "Union..." handwritten in yellow; the laws/courts/church icons pop around Scotland.
- A speech bubble "Wait… that was an option?!" from Wales with eyes.
- A red "BRIBED" stamp slams onto the Irish parliament; coins fly.
- Easter Rising / War of Independence as small labelled stickers; Ireland turns orange and breaks away; only NI stays red.
- The full name builds up word by word in serif with glowing highlights.
- People icons in four colours, "one nation" / "Four countries", then the UK flashes white and becomes one red "1"
  with "Sovereign State".

**Pace:** a new visual event about every 1-1.5 s; the camera never stops (slow push, pan, dive); motion is always on the
map. Captions are tiny, white, centre-low. No host, no loop (it ends on "sovereign state" and cuts).

## Fact check

| Claim | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| One king, one passport, one army | **True.** One monarch, one British passport, one British Armed Forces. |
| Four countries, each with its own capital | **True** (London, Edinburgh, Cardiff, Belfast). |
| 13th century: Wales "joined", i.e. was conquered, and ringed with castles | **True.** Edward I's conquest (1277-1283) and his castles (Caernarfon, Conwy, Harlech, Beaumaris: the "Iron Ring"). Wales was legally annexed later, by the Laws in Wales Acts 1535-1542. |
| The Scots fought back for centuries; in 1603 their king inherited the English throne and moved to London | **True.** The Wars of Scottish Independence; the 1603 Union of the Crowns (James VI of Scotland became James I of England). |
| Scotland, struggling financially, agreed to a full union but kept its own laws, courts and church | **True.** The Darien scheme (a failed colony in Panama, 1698-1700) lost a large share of Scotland's capital; the 1707 Acts of Union kept Scots law, courts and the Church of Scotland. |
| That's how Great Britain was formed | **True** (1 May 1707). |
| Ireland kept its own parliament; in 1801 it was bought with titles and money and voted for union | **True in substance.** The Act of Union 1800 (in force 1 January 1801) passed the Irish Parliament after peerages, offices and about £1.26 million in compensation to borough owners; widely described as bribery. |
| After the Easter Rising and the War of Independence most of the island broke away; only the north remained | **True.** Easter Rising 1916; War of Independence 1919-1921; Irish Free State 1922; Northern Ireland opted out (December 1922). |
| Full name: United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland | **True** (since 1927). |
| Four countries, one sovereign state | **True.** |

Extra facts for our version (checked):
- **Cricket:** the England team is run by the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB); Scotland has its own team; the
  Ireland team represents the whole island.
- **Football:** England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are separate FIFA members.
- **Olympics:** one team, Team GB (Great Britain and Northern Ireland).
- **Partition:** Ireland was partitioned by a UK act, the Government of Ireland Act 1920 (Northern Ireland created 1921);
  India by another, the Indian Independence Act 1947.
- **"About 200 years":** Company rule from 1757 (Plassey) and Crown rule 1858-1947: about 190 years; "करीब 200 साल" is
  the usual phrasing and is hedged.

## Our angle (Mumbai formula)

An Indian viewer's link: **the Angrez who ruled us** are themselves four countries, and **one parliament was bought**.
Everyday proof Indians know: **cricket** (England team = England and Wales). A people beat: the Irish fight, and the
**partition parallel** (Ireland 1921, India 1947, both by acts in London). Visual hook from the reference: kinetic type
from frame 0 and the white-flash reveal of the four countries, rebuilt in our house style with India in the frame.
