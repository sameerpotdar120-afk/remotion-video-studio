Jin Angrezon ne hum par kareeb 200 saal raj kiya, unka UK sach mein chaar desh hai. Aur inmein se ek ki Parliament 1801 mein bribe se khareedi gayi thi. Kaise?

UK mein ek King, ek Passport, ek Army. Par andar chaar desh: England, Scotland, Wales aur Northern Ireland. Chaaron ki apni Capital.

Vishwas nahi ho raha? Cricket mein "England" ki team asal mein England aur Wales ki hai. Football mein chaaron alag-alag khelte hain. Aur Olympics mein sab ek: Team GB.

Shuruaat England se hui. 13vi sadi mein England ne Wales ko jeet liya, aur use bade-bade kilon ke ghere mein baandh diya.

Phir baari Scotland ki. Scots sadiyon tak lade. Aur 1603 mein Scotland ka Raja England ki gaddi ka bhi vaaris bana, aur London chala gaya.

Upar se, Panama mein colony basane ki fail koshish mein Scotland ka bahut paisa doob gaya. To 1707 mein wo poore milan ke liye taiyaar hua, par apne kanoon, adalatein aur church bacha liye. Aise bana Great Britain.

Ireland par bhi Angrezon ka control tha, par uski apni Parliament thi. 1801 mein wo Parliament khitabon aur paison se khareedi gayi, aur usne milan ke liye vote diya. Aise bana United Kingdom.

Par Irish log khush nahi the. 1916 ka Easter Rising, phir azaadi ki jung. Aur 1922 mein zyadatar Ireland alag ho gaya, bas uttar ka hissa reh gaya.

Ireland ka batwara London ki Parliament ke kanoon se hua. Aur 1947 mein Bharat ka bhi.

Isiliye aaj poora naam hai: United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland.

Yaani chaar desh, par ek sovereign rashtra. Jude zaroor, par kabhi ek Nation nahi bane. To agli baar koi sabko Angrez kahe, to yaad rakhna...
