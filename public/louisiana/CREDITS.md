Facts: Louisiana Purchase 828,000 sq mi / 2,140,000 km² (≈ 65% of India's 3,287,263 km²), $15M for the territory after a $10M offer for New Orleans, under 3 cents an acre, nearly doubled the US (Wikipedia; US National Archives; Lewis & Clark Journals). Saint-Domingue ~500,000 enslaved by 1790; the Leclerc expedition (~31,000 troops) lost more than two-thirds of its men, mostly to yellow fever.
