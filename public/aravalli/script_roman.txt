Rashtrapati Bhavan ek pahaad par khada hai. Aur wahi pahaad, Delhi ko Desert banne se rokta hai.

Ye hai Aravalli. Gujarat se Delhi tak, kareeb 700 kilometer lambi, aur kareeb 2 arab saal purani. Himalaya to sirf kareeb 5 crore saal purana hai.

Rashtrapati Bhavan jis Raisina Pahaadi par hai, wo isi Aravalli ka aakhri sira hai.

Iske West me Thar Desert. East me hare-bhare Maidan. Aur beech me khadi hai Aravalli, ek Deewar ki tarah.

Par ye Deewar toot rahi hai. 2018 me Supreme Court ke saamne Report aayi: Rajasthan me check ki gayi 128 me se 31 pahaadiyan, 50 saal me gayab ho chuki thi. Kaaran, Mining.

Jahan pahaad gaye, wahan Deewar me kareeb 12 badi Daraarein ban gayi. Aur inhi raaston se Thar ki dhool aage badhti hai.

Fir 2025 me Supreme Court ne ek nayi Definition maani: 100 metre se oonchi pahaadi hi Aravalli maani jayegi.

Reports ke mutabik, 20 metre se oonchi kareeb 12,000 pahaadiyon me se, sirf kareeb 1,000 hi 100 metre se oonchi hain.

Virodh hua, aur December 2025 me Court ne apna hi Decision rok diya.

To agli baar Rashtrapati Bhavan dekho, to yaad rakhna...
