Rashtrapati Bhavan ek pahaad par khada hai. Aur wahi pahaad, Delhi ko registan banne se rokta hai.

Ye hai Aravalli. Gujarat se Delhi tak, kareeb 700 kilometer lambi, aur kareeb 2 arab saal purani. Himalaya toh sirf kareeb 5 crore saal purana hai.

Rashtrapati Bhavan jis Raisina pahaadi par hai, wo isi Aravalli ka aakhri sira hai.

Iske pashchim mein Thar ka registan. Purab mein hare-bhare maidan. Aur beech mein khadi hai Aravalli, ek deewar ki tarah.

Par ye deewar toot rahi hai. 2018 mein Supreme Court ke saamne report aayi: Rajasthan mein Aravalli ki 128 mein se 31 pahaadiyan, 50 saal mein gaayab ho chuki thi. Wajah, khanan.

Jahan pahaad gaye, wahan deewar mein kareeb 12 badi daraarein ban gayi. Aur inhi raaston se Thar ki dhool aage badhti hai.

Phir 2025 mein Supreme Court ne ek nayi paribhasha maani: 100 meter se oonchi pahaadi hi Aravalli maani jayegi.

Reports ke mutabik, 20 meter se oonchi kareeb 12,000 pahaadiyon mein se, sirf kareeb 1,000 hi 100 meter se oonchi hain.

Virodh hua, aur December 2025 mein Court ne apna hi faisla rok diya.

Toh agli baar Rashtrapati Bhavan dekho, toh yaad rakhna...
