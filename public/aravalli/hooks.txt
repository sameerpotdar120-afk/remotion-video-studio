Rashtrapati Bhavan ek pahaad par khada hai. Aur wahi pahaad Delhi ko registan banne se rokta hai.
Tum Delhi ki jis dhool mein saans lete ho, wo Thar se aati hai, ek tooti hui deewar ke raaste.
Rajasthan mein 31 pahaadiyan 50 saal mein gaayab ho gayi. Kahan gayi?
Himalaya ke paida hone se 2 arab saal pehle se ek pahaad Delhi ki raksha kar raha hai.
Delhi aur registan ke beech sirf ek deewar hai, aur wo deewar toot rahi hai.
