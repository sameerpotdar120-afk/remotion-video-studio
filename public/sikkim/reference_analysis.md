# Reference analysis: "Sikkim will get its first-ever railway station in 2027"

**Source:** @The.informed.citizen.1. 720×1280, 24 fps, 63.3 s, Hindi VO with no presenter on camera (his face appears only on the follow card at the end).

**How I checked it:** the whole video at 2 fps, then 4–5 fps through every move and transition (0–5 s, 3–12 s, 10–16 s, 16–26 s, 33–44 s, 44–52 s, 59–63 s), full-resolution stills of five key frames, a Whisper transcript, and loudness and onset measurements of the soundtrack.

## The big difference from our usual references

This one is **half map, half "real" footage**. About 29 s is the map, about 22 s is aerial drone-style video (AI-looking) of a mountain railway with graphics drawn over it, about 8 s is a screen-recording of an article, and the last 3 s is the creator's follow card. The map carries the hook and the "why"; the footage carries the "how hard it is to build".

There are fewer events than in our last two references: about 40 in 63 s (one every ~1.6 s). Instead of many small pops, each shot has **one strong, slow-building graphic**: the rail network, the glowing state, the gauge, the traced route, the tinted mountain, the labelled tunnel.

## Look

- **Map:** Esri World Imagery, darkened to about 40% and desaturated, warm brown plains and grey-white snow, a strong black vignette on all edges and a dark sky band at the top. The credit "Tiles © Esri — Source: Esri, i-cubed, USDA, USGS, AEX…" runs along the bottom. India's outline includes Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.
- **Highlight colour:** one accent, cyan-teal (#2EE6E0 glow, #0E6D74 deep), used for the state fill, the 3D title and the gauge. Gold is used only for the opening network; pink only for the traced route; red only for the earthquake rings.
- **Typography:** bold condensed sans in caps for titles (3D, bevelled, glowing); small white caps for labels on dark bars.
- **Camera:** always moving, slow, smooth pushes, never a still frame; one big swing from India to Sikkim.
- **Footage grade:** cool, misty, slightly desaturated greens; the forest train shot at the end is much brighter and greener (a lift for the ending).
- **No subtitles** on the video. The VO carries it, and the on-screen text is only labels.

## Sound

- **VO:** a natural male Hindi voice, conversational ("देखो", "दोस्तो"), fast and even, about 2.9 words per second.
- **Music:** a dark, cinematic, pulsing bed (low drones and a steady low beat), mixed **loud**. In the two pauses (9.4–10.2 s and 35.7–36.5 s) it sits at about −16.5 dB RMS, the same as the speech (−17.3 dB), so the music never really ducks.
- **SFX:** whooshes and hits on the transitions and pops: 0.7 and 0.9 s (network lines), 2.2–2.5 (lines out), 4.8 (Sikkim lights up), 6.0 and 6.8 (title, train), 11.6 (Himalayas), 13.6 and 15.3 (gauge, badges), 16.4 (cut to footage), 19.7–20.2 (route trace), 22.8 (morph), 27.5, 31.2, 32.8–34.2 (the tear and clouds), 37.9–38.2 (thunder, card), 46.4 and 47.2 (article), 52.4 (train cut), 58.7–61.9 (follow card).

## What we'd swap in (copyright-free only)

- **Esri basemap:** our EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless with AWS terrain, graded dark the same way, with Natural Earth's India edition for **India's official boundary**.
- **Drone and train footage:** we can't use theirs, or Indian Railways or news footage. Options are AI video clips (Omni, the way we make Null's clips: viaduct, tunnel, peak, train) or the creator's own AI images with our code-drawn route trace, tint and push.
- **The article screenshot:** we can't show a real publication's page. We could build our own "fact card" in code with a highlight sweep, using our own words.
- **The follow card with a face:** our videos loop and don't end on a CTA, and Null never appears at the start or end. So this part would change unless you decide otherwise.
- **The 3D title, gauge, hex badges, rail network, track with train, storm card, pulse rings and tunnel labels:** all buildable in Remotion.

## Facts mentioned (to check before we script)

- Sikkim is the only Indian state without a railway station (true today; the nearest stations are New Jalpaiguri and Siliguri).
- The Sevoke–Rangpo line (about 45 km, mostly tunnels and bridges) is under construction; Rangpo will be the first station in Sikkim.
- The reference says the line could open by December 2027. That's their claim; we'd re-check the latest official date before using it.
- "More than 60% of Sikkim is high mountains and deep valleys" is their figure, and I haven't verified it.
