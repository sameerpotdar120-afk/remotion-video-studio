**1. The Question: 74, STRONG** (used)
"You never notice one second. But in it, 4 babies are born and Earth flies 30 km. What else?"

**2. The Question: 73, STRONG**
"You just lost one second. In it, Earth flew 30 km and 4 babies were born. But what did India do?"
```
SPECIFICITY 91 · ADDRESS 76 · STAKES 62 · CURIOSITY 76 · weakest: STAKES
```
Winner formula: **The Question**. Two concrete numbers in the first breath prove the payoff exists, and "और क्या होता
है?" opens the list the rest of the video fills. "30 km" comes back at the end as the twist: you moved too.
