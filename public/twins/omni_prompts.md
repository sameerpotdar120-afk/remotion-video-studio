# Twin countries: Omni prompts for Null

Null appears once, on the last line only, for about 4 seconds in the bottom half of a split screen (the mirror map stays in the top half):

> तो सोचो, आपकी Copy कौन है? ये Video उसी को भेजो।

Make one 10-second clip. I'll cut the best 4 seconds from it and play our VO over it (the Omni audio is muted).

## How to generate
- Tool: Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds.
- Starting frame: attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg` every time.
- Make 2–3 takes of the main clip. If it's blocked or the gestures come out wrong, use the backup prompt.
- Name the files `null_twins_A1.mp4`, `null_twins_A2.mp4`, and so on, and send them with the VO.

## Clip A: "आपकी Copy कौन है? … उसी को भेजो" (main)
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He is asking his audience a playful question:
0–3 s: he sits relaxed with his hands on the desk, then lifts his right hand beside the microphone and points his index finger straight at the camera, as if asking "you".
3–6 s: he turns that hand into an open palm facing up and tilts his head slightly to one side, a curious "who is it?" gesture.
6–10 s: he makes a smooth sweeping motion with his right hand out to the side, as if passing something along to a friend, then rests his hand back on the desk.

Smooth, slow, confident movements, with his hands beside the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```

## Clip B: backup (shorter, if A is blocked)
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He points one finger at the camera, then opens his palm with a slight head tilt, then sweeps his hand to the side as if passing something to a friend, and rests it on the desk. Smooth and slow, hands beside the microphone.
```

## What I check when it comes back
- The face design stays steady (shape, gold line, eye glow).
- The gloves keep five fingers, and the mic doesn't move.
- The Gemini sparkle sits in the bottom right as before. The split-screen crop around the mask and hands drops it.
