# Topic after Everest: "भारत की आधी आबादी, नक्शे के सिर्फ़ 10% हिस्से में"

## Why
- The most repeated breakout pattern in English map Shorts right now: "almost nobody lives here" / "most people live here".
  Geography By Geoff's "America's Empty Belt" did 7.5M (130× his median) and "Almost Nobody Lives On Colombia's West Coast"
  33×; earlier Mini Maps "Why do 95% of Chinese people live in this area?" 57×. Nobody has done India in Hindi.
- Viewers find their own place on the map (the Mumbai formula), so they comment and send it: "तुम पीले में हो या नीले में?"
- Zero political or communal angle: people, land, rivers.

## The numbers (our own calculation, 24 Nov 2026)
Data: WorldPop, Global 1 km population, 2020, UN-adjusted, India (CC BY 4.0, www.worldpop.org). Each 1 km cell's area is
corrected for latitude; cells are sorted from densest to emptiest and summed.

| Share of India's people | Lives on this share of the land |
|---|---|
| 25 % | **1.7 %** |
| 50 % | **9.4 %** |
| 75 % | 27.0 % |
| 90 % | 51.2 % |

- The emptiest 18.3 % of the land holds only 1 % of people; the emptiest 35.4 % holds 5 %.
- Total in the grid: 138.0 crore (the UN's 2020 figure, which the dataset is scaled to), on 31.44 lakh km² of mapped land.
- The "half" area means places with more than about 980 people per km². 53 % of that half lives inside the Ganga plain
  (23–31°N, 74–89°E). The rest is coastal Kerala, Tamil Nadu and the big cities.
- Census cross-check (2011): UP, Bihar and West Bengal together hold about 32.6 % of Indians on about 13 % of India's area.
  That's consistent.

## Wording rules
- Say "करीब 10 %" and "2020 के data के हिसाब से". The grid covers the land India administers (31.4 lakh km²); India's
  official area is 32.87 lakh km² (on which the half-population share is about 9.0 %). "करीब 10 %" holds either way.
- Today's population is higher (UN about 146 crore in 2025), but the shares barely move. Use shares, not "138 crore".
- India's official boundary on every map (Natural Earth India edition).

- "भारत की आधी आबादी, नक्शे के सिर्फ़ इस हिस्से में रहती है।" Frame 0: India dark, the gold belt already lit.
