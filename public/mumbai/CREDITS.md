Facts: seven islands (Colaba, Old Woman's Island, Bombay, Mazagaon, Parel, Worli, Mahim); Portuguese from 1534 (Treaty of Bassein);
dowry of Catherine of Braganza to Charles II, 1661; leased to the East India Company in 1668 for £10 a year; Governor William
Hornby began the Hornby Vellard in 1782 against the Company's wishes (a suspension order came, he carried on), finished 1784,
closing the Great Breach at Worli; Mumbai Coastal Road opened in 2024 on about 100+ hectares of reclaimed land; Worli Koliwada
fishers protested the loss of fishing grounds.
