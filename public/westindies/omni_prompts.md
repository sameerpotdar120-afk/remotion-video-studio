**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** the file name above each clip (2–3 takes each, e.g. `null_wi_B1.mp4`, `null_wi_B2.mp4`).

```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He explains a surprising fact:
0–3 s: he raises one finger as if saying "and here is the strange part", holds it, then lowers it.

6–10 s: he presses one flat hand down on the desk as if pointing to solid ground, taps it twice, then rests his hands.

Smooth, calm, explanatory movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
