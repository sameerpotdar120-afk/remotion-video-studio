# Reference analysis: "You Can't Find West Indies on the Map, Here's Why" (DecodingSolo)

720×1280, 30 fps, 46.6 s, Hindi voiceover. Analysed at 4 frames per second throughout and at every frame (30 fps)
around the key transitions (0.0, 1.2, 10.4, 14.3, 15.1, 28.6, 34.1, 37.4, 42.4 s). Transcripts:
`reference_transcript_hi.txt` (original Hindi) and `reference_transcript_en.txt` (English pass, used for the list of
countries, which the Hindi pass collapsed).

## The look ("dark theme")

- **Base map:** a flat Web Mercator satellite map with no labels and no borders. The sea is a very dark navy-teal
  (close to #0A1A22), the land is the satellite colour but slightly darkened and desaturated (olive, tan), and
  shallow banks (the Bahamas, the Cuban shelf) glow turquoise against the dark sea. Polar ice is near-white.
- **Bathymetry:** at island zooms you can see faint seabed relief in the dark water (trenches, shelf edges).
- **Grade:** dark, low-key, cool. No vignette to speak of, no grain, no light leaks. The only bright colours on
  screen are the fills, the flags and the props.
- **Fills:** each country the VO names gets a flat, saturated, semi-transparent fill (about 80% opacity, the
  satellite texture still shows through) in its own colour: Jamaica turquoise, Haiti green, Dominican Republic
  orange, Barbados turquoise, Trinidad and Tobago red, Grenada olive yellow, Saint Lucia turquoise, Antigua red with
  a blue halo, Dominica green, Saint Kitts and Nevis blue, Saint Vincent gold, Cuba red. A slightly darker rim and a
  soft outer glow on some (Antigua's halo).
- **Labels:** the country name sits inside its own fill, in the fill's colour but lighter, in a rounded sans
  (Poppins/Montserrat-like, semi-bold), no outline. Long names wrap ("Saint Kitts and / Nevis" set at an angle along
  the island chain, "Saint Vincent and / the Grenadines" in a muted teal above the island).
- **Outlines:** a thin white line (about 3 px, slight glow) draws the region boundary (0:09–0:10 the Caribbean as a
  box-ish polygon; 0:42.5–0:45 a rounded hull around the islands that stays as the camera pulls out to the globe).
- **Watermark:** "DecodingSolo" small, grey, bottom centre, the whole video.
- **No subtitles** on screen at all.

## Motion

- **Camera:** continuous, smooth zooms and pans on the flat map, ease-in-out, about 0.5–1 s per move, often chained
  with no hold. Big whip moves between continents (0:02.0 → 0:03.0 out from India to Africa, 0:08.0 → 0:08.75
  across the Atlantic). No rotation, no tilt, no 3D.
- **Island tour (0:14.5–0:27):** a hard cut every 0.5–1 s to a tight zoom on the next island, each fill already
  growing as the cut lands. This is the fastest stretch: 12 islands in about 13 s.
- **Fill reveals (micro-frames):** Haiti's fill wipes in from left to right in about 4 frames (0:15.20–0:15.27)
  with a soft leading edge; Jamaica's fades in over about 6 frames; the Dominican Republic's grows out from the
  border with Haiti.
- **Props:** cut-out PNGs with no shadow: Chris Gayle (fades in over about 8 frames at 0:01.3, then at 0:28.7 he
  rises out of Jamaica, bottom-masked by the island), the West Indies cricket crest on a maroon card (pops in, an
  arrow curves from India to it, 0:02–0:03), a shrug emoji above the crest (0:06.75), a magnifying glass that
  glides across the world map searching (0:04.3–0:06.3), flag-filled country shapes (Jamaica, Haiti, Dominican
  Republic on the overview, Spain at 0:33), Columbus as a portrait cut-out riding a small ship (0:33–0:41).
- **Dark transition (0:34.2–0:34.5):** the map fades almost to black in 8 frames as the voyage starts, so the
  Atlantic crossing is Columbus and his ship on near-black, a thin yellow route line trailing behind; the map fades
  back in under him as he reaches the Bahamas (0:36.2–0:37).
- **Speech bubble (0:37.5–0:38.0):** builds in 15 frames: a vertical tick, then the tail, then the yellow bar grows
  sideways, then the text "Is This India ?" types in.
- **Title card:** "1452 CE" (white, small caps) on the Atlantic at 0:32.25 (the reference's own error: the voice
  says 1492).
- **End (0:42.5–0:46.5):** pull out from the Caribbean to the whole of North America and Greenland with the white
  outline around the islands, no CTA card. The video ends on the map.

## Sound

- A music bed runs the whole video and sits fairly high: about 6–9 dB under the voice in pauses (gaps measure −19 to
  −24 dB against a voice median of −15 dB).
- Short hits on the cuts and fills: the loudest gap reading (−12 dB at 0:15.5) lines up with the Haiti and
  Dominican Republic fills, so the fills carry a pop. Fast whooshes sit on the big camera moves.
- One Hindi voice, fast and flat, about 3.3 words per second.

## What the reference gets wrong (so our version can be better)

- It lists Cuba, Haiti, the Dominican Republic and the Bahamas as part of "the West Indies" Chris Gayle plays for.
  The cricket team (Cricket West Indies) doesn't include any of them. It is made up mostly of former British
  colonies, and it includes Guyana, which isn't an island at all but a country on the South American mainland.
- "14 countries" doesn't match its own list (13 names). The West Indies as a region has 13 sovereign island
  countries plus many territories (British, French, Dutch, US).
- The title card says 1452; Columbus sailed in 1492.
- It never mentions that the West Indies really was one country: the West Indies Federation, 1958–1962.
