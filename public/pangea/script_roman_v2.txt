Tumhe mehsoos nahi hota, par hamara Bharat har saal kareeb 5 centimeter khisak raha hai. Aur 25 crore saal baad, yahi safar mammals ko mita sakta hai. Kyu?
Kareeb 30 crore saal pehle, dharti ki saari zameen ek hi tukda thi: Pangea.
Aur Bharat? Wo Asia mein tha hi nahi. Africa, Antarctica aur Australia se chipka hua tha.
Phir Pangea toota, aur Bharat ban gaya duniya ka sabse tez bhaagta tukda: har saal 15 centimeter se bhi zyada.
Beech mein tha ek samandar: Tethys.
Kareeb 5 crore saal pehle, Bharat Asia se ja takraya. Samandar ka farsh upar uth gaya, aur bana Himalaya.
Isiliye Everest ki choti par aaj bhi samandar ke jeevon ke fossil milte hain.
Aur jo shaligram hum ghar mein poojte hain? Wo bhi usi samandar ke jeev, ammonite, ke fossil hain.
Aur ye takkar aaj bhi jaari hai.
Ek model kehta hai: Atlantic sikud jayega, America phir Africa se ja milega, aur Australia Asia se.
25 crore saal baad banega naya supercontinent: Pangaea Ultima.
2023 ki ek study kehti hai: uska zyadatar hissa 40 se 70 degree tak tapega, aur mammals ke rehne layak bachegi bas 8 se 16 pratishat zameen.
Yani jis takkar ne humein Himalaya diya, wahi ek din humein mita sakti hai. Aur ye safar abhi chal raha hai, kyunki...
