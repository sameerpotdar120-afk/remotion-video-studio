Tumhe mehsoos nahi hota, par Bharat har saal kareeb 5 centimeter khisak raha hai. Aur 25 crore saal baad, saare continents fir se takraakar ek ho jaayenge. Tab shaayad mammals bach hi na paayein. Kyu?

Ye tha Pangea. Kareeb 30 crore saal pehle, dharti ki saari zameen ek hi tukda thi.

Aur Bharat? Africa, Antarctica aur Australia se chipka hua.

Fir Pangea toota. Bharat uttar ki or bhaaga, aur kareeb 5 crore saal pehle Asia se ja takraaya. Usi takkar se bana Himalaya.

Aur ye takkar aaj bhi jaari hai. Himalaya har saal kareeb 1 centimeter upar uth raha hai.

Ab aage kya? Ek model ke hisaab se, Atlantic sikud jaayega, aur America fir se Africa aur Europe se ja milega.

Australia, Asia se takraayega.

Aur kareeb 25 crore saal baad, banega ek naya supercontinent.

2023 ki ek study kehti hai: uska zyaadatar hissa 40 se 70 degree tak tapega.

Aur mammals ke rehne laayak bachegi, bas 8 se 16 pratishat zameen.

Yaani jo zameen sabko jodegi, wahi humein mita sakti hai. Aur ye safar abhi chal raha hai, kyunki...
