Tumhe mehsoos nahi hota, par hamara Bharat har saal kareeb 5 centimeter khisak raha hai. Aur 25 crore saal baad, saare continents phir se takrakar ek ho jayenge. Tab shayad mammals bach hi na paayein. Kyu?

Ye tha Pangea. Kareeb 30 crore saal pehle, dharti ki saari zameen ek hi tukda thi.

Aur Bharat? Africa, Antarctica aur Australia se chipka hua.

Phir Pangea toota. Bharat uttar ki or bhaaga, aur kareeb 5 crore saal pehle Asia se ja takraya. Usi takkar se bana Himalaya.

Aur ye takkar aaj bhi jaari hai. Himalaya har saal kareeb 1 centimeter upar uth raha hai.

Ab aage kya? Ek model ke hisaab se, Atlantic sikud jayega, aur America phir se Africa aur Europe se ja milega.

Australia, Asia se takrayega.

Aur kareeb 25 crore saal baad, banega ek naya supercontinent: Pangaea Ultima.

2023 ki ek study kehti hai: uska zyadatar hissa 40 se 70 degree tak tapega.

Aur mammals ke rehne layak bachegi, bas 8 se 16 pratishat zameen.

Yani jo zameen sabko jodegi, wahi hume mita sakti hai. Aur ye safar abhi chal raha hai, kyunki...
