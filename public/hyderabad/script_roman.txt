Tum aaj Hyderabad ki Biryani khaate ho, par 1948 tak Hyderabad Bharat mein tha hi nahi.

Uska apna Raja tha, apni fauj, apna sikka. Aur wo Raja duniya ka sabse ameer aadmi kehlata tha.

Ye thi Hyderabad Riyasat, Bharat ke theek beech mein. Kareeb 1.6 crore log, jinmein 80% se zyada Hindu.

Par Raja, Nizam Mir Osman Ali Khan, ko chahiye tha Bharat ke beechon-beech ek azaad desh.

Phir uthe Razakar, Qasim Rizvi ki hathiyaarband fauj. Gaon-gaon mein darr aur hinsa.

13 September 1948. Sardar Patel ke faisle par Bharatiya Sena Hyderabad mein daakhil hui: Operation Polo.

Sirf kareeb 108 ghante, aur 17 September ko Nizam ki fauj ne hathiyaar daal diye.

Aur wahi Nizam? 1950 mein Bharat ke Samvidhan ke tahat Hyderabad Rajya ke Rajpramukh bane.

Par kahani yahan khatam nahi hui. Haar ke sirf 3 din baad, Hyderabad ke khazaane se kareeb 10 lakh Pound, London mein Pakistan ke High Commissioner ke bank account mein pahunch gaye.

71 saal case chala. 2019 mein London ki High Court ne kaha: ye paisa Nizam ke vaariso aur Bharat ka hai. Tab tak wo badhkar 3.5 crore Pound ho chuka tha.

Toh agli baar Charminar dekho, toh yaad rakhna...
