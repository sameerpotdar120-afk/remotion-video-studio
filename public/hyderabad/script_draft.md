## Fact notes (checked 17 November 2026)

- **"1948 तक हैदराबाद भारत में था ही नहीं":** Hyderabad did not accede in 1947 (a Standstill Agreement in November
  1947); it came under Indian control after Operation Polo in September 1948.
- **"अपनी फ़ौज, अपना सिक्का":** the State had its own army and its own currency (the Hyderabadi rupee).
- **"दुनिया का सबसे अमीर आदमी कहलाता था":** TIME's cover of 22 February 1937 called the Nizam "the richest man in the
  world". Hence "कहलाता था", not a ranking.
- **"185 carat… paperweight":** the Jacob Diamond (about 185 carats), widely reported as his paperweight; on screen with
  "कहते हैं".
- **"करीब 1.6 करोड़ लोग, 80% से ज़्यादा हिंदू":** 1941 census 16,338,534 people; sources give 81–85% Hindu.
- **"रज़ाकार, क़ासिम रिज़वी":** the Razakars, the armed militia led by Qasim Razvi.
- **"13 सितंबर 1948… Operation Polo… करीब 108 घंटे… 17 सितंबर":** began 13 September; the Nizam announced a ceasefire on
  17 September (the formal surrender of the State forces is dated 17 or 18 September in different sources).
- **"1950 में… राजप्रमुख":** Mir Osman Ali Khan was Rajpramukh of Hyderabad State from 26 January 1950 to 31 October 1956.
- **"हार के सिर्फ़ 3 दिन बाद… करीब 10 लाख pound… Pakistan के High Commissioner":** on 20 September 1948, £1,007,940
  was moved from a Hyderabad government account at Westminster Bank, London, to an account in the name of Habib Ibrahim
  Rahimtoola, Pakistan's High Commissioner (by the former finance minister, Moin Nawaz Jung; his authority was
  disputed, which is why the VO says "हैदराबाद के ख़ज़ाने से", not "निज़ाम ने भेजे").
- **"71 साल… 2019… निज़ाम के वारिसों और भारत का… 3.5 करोड़ pound":** High Court of England and Wales (Marcus Smith J),
  2 October 2019: the fund, about £35 million, belongs to those claiming through Nizam VII (his heirs and India);
  Pakistan's claim dismissed.
- **"सरदार पटेल के फ़ैसले पर":** Patel, as Home Minister, drove the decision to send the army. Nehru's government
  approved it; the army was led by Major General J. N. Chaudhuri.
