Hyderabad, azaadi ke 13 mahine baad tak Bharat ka hissa hi nahi tha.
Tum aaj Hyderabad mein biryani khaate ho, par 1948 tak wo Bharat mein tha hi nahi.
Duniya ka sabse ameer aadmi heere ko paperweight banata tha, aur Bharat ke beech mein apna desh chahta tha.
Hyderabad ka paisa 71 saal tak London ki ek bank mein Pakistan ke naam par phansa raha.
Charminar wala Hyderabad, 1948 tak apna sikka, apni fauj aur apna Raja rakhta tha. Bharat ka hissa nahi tha.
