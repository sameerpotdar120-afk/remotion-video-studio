1947 mein Gujarat ka ek hissa Pakistan mein chala gaya tha, aur uske saath Somnath Mandir bhi. Pakistan ka Map aaj bhi use apna dikhata hai. Kaise?

Ye hai Junagadh. Aaj Gujarat ka ek Zila, par 1947 mein ek Princely State. Yahan ke kareeb 80% log Hindu the, par Raja tha Nawab Mahabat Khan.

Azaadi ke waqt har Princely State ko chunna tha: Bharat ya Pakistan. Aur 15 September 1947 ko Nawab ne Pakistan chun liya.

Par Junagadh ki zameen Pakistan se judi bhi nahi thi. Teen taraf Bharat, aur saamne Samundar.

Phir aaye Sardar Patel. Bharat ne Junagadh ki nakabandi kar di. Mumbai mein janta ki apni Sarkar bani, "Aarzi Hukumat", aur usne gaon-gaon apne control mein le liye.

October mein Nawab Karachi bhaag gaya.

9 November 1947 ko, Nawab ke Diwan ke kehne par, Bharat ne Junagadh apne haath mein le liya. Aur wo Diwan the Shah Nawaz Bhutto, yaani aage chalkar Pakistan ke Prime Minister bane Zulfikar Ali Bhutto ke pita.

13 November ko Patel Somnath pahunche. Mandir khandhar tha. Wahin ise phir se banane ka faisla hua, aur 1951 mein naya Somnath Mandir khula.

February 1948 mein janta se poocha gaya: Bharat ya Pakistan? Junagadh mein 1,90,870 Vote pade. Pakistan ke liye sirf 91.

Phir bhi, 2020 mein Pakistan ne apna naya Map jaari kiya, aur usmein Junagadh ko apna dikhaya.

Yaani jis Somnath ke darshan tum aaj bina Passport karte ho, wo ek Nawab ke ek Signature se kaagaz par Pakistan ka ho chuka tha. Aur Pakistan ka Map aaj bhi kehta hai...
