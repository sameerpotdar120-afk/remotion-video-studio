Tumhari jeb mein Bharat ka ek naksha chhupa hai. Bas note palat ke dekho.
500 ka note palto, peeche Lal Qila hai. Par 2000 ke note wali jagah Bharat mein hai hi nahi.
Tumne 100 ka note hazaar baar dekha hai, par uske peeche Gujarat ki jo baoli hai, kabhi dekhi?
Agar tum har note ke peeche wali jagah ghoom lo, to tum aadha Bharat ghoom loge.
Tumhare 10 ke note ke peeche kareeb 800 saal purana ek Surya Mandir hai.
