Tumne 100 ka note hazaar baar dekha hai, par uske peeche Gujarat ki jo baoli hai, kabhi dekhi?

Ye hai Rani ki Vav, Patan mein. Kareeb 1000 saal purani, 7 manzil gehri seedhiyon wali baoli.

Aur sirf 100 nahi. Har note ke peeche Bharat ki ek jagah hai.

10 ke note par Odisha ka Konark Surya Mandir. 20 par Maharashtra ki Ellora ki Gufaayein. 50 par Karnataka ke Hampi ka Patthar ka Rath. 200 par Madhya Pradesh ka Sanchi Stupa. Aur 500 par Delhi ka Lal Qila.

Ab twist: ye chhah ki chhah jagah UNESCO World Heritage Sites hain. Yaani tumhari jeb mein, Odisha se Gujarat tak, Bharat ka ek poora naksha ghoom raha hai.

Aur 2000 ka note? Uske peeche koi imaarat nahi thi. Uske peeche tha Mangalyaan, jo 2014 mein pehli hi koshish mein Mangal tak pahuncha. Aisa karne wala duniya ka pehla desh: Bharat.

Wo note RBI wapas le chuka hai, par aaj bhi legal tender hai.

To agli baar koi tumse 100 ka note maange, to bas itna poochna...
