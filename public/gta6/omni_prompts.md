# GTA 6 Short: Omni prompts for Null (two short clips)

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds.

**Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`.

**Save as:** the file name above each clip, in `public/gta6/null/`. Make 2 takes each if you can, e.g.
`null_gta_A1.mp4`, `null_gta_A2.mp4`.

Null appears in the middle only, in split screen, for about 3 s each (about 6–7 s in total). He never appears at the
start or the end, because the video loops. Null is our own character: he mimes a generic gamer and never holds or
shows anything from GTA (no logos, no game screens).

**null_gta_A.mp4** (beat 4, the line "Wahi game, jo hum me se kitnon ne pehli baar cyber cafe me khela tha, bees rupaye ghanta dekar.")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He remembers playing games at a cyber cafe as a kid:
0–3 s: he leans in toward the camera, one hand on the desk moving as if gripping and clicking a computer mouse, eyes focused.
3–5 s: he jerks back and laughs, slapping the desk once, as if he just pulled off something wild in a game.
5–8 s: he pats his shirt pocket and holds up two fingers with a sheepish grin, as if counting out a few coins to pay.
8–10 s: he rests both hands calmly on the desk, still smiling.

Natural, playful movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
Backup:
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He leans in clicking an imaginary mouse, jerks back laughing and slaps the desk once, pats his shirt pocket and holds up two fingers with a sheepish grin, then rests his hands.
```

**null_gta_B.mp4** (beat 8, the line "Phir terah saal ka intezaar. School se job tak pahunch gayi ek poori generation.")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He shows a very long wait:
0–3 s: he looks at his wrist as if checking a watch, then taps it twice, impatient.
3–6 s: he holds one flat hand low near the desk and raises it slowly up to head height, as if showing a child growing into an adult.
6–8 s: he lets out a big tired sigh, shoulders dropping, and shakes his head with a small smile.
8–10 s: he rests both hands calmly on the desk.

Clear, deliberate movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
Backup:
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He checks an imaginary wristwatch and taps it twice, raises a flat hand slowly from desk height to head height as if showing someone growing up, sighs with dropping shoulders and a small smile, then rests his hands.
```
