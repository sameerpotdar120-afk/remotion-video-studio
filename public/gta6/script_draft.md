# GTA 6 ka Vice Beach, Bengaluru mein: Hindi Short (draft v1, 22 Nov 2026)

Reference: a 69 s English Short (720×1280, 30 fps, 5 hard cuts). GTA's history on a US satellite map: each game's
fictional city wipes in red over the real city it copies (Vice City → Miami, San Andreas → California + Nevada,
Liberty City → New York, Los Santos → Los Angeles), a sales counter climbs as stacks of game boxes, and it ends on
GTA VI's release date with "how many copies will it sell?". About 100% map, continuous camera, a change every 0.5–1 s.

Our version: the same map history, but opened and closed on India. Rockstar's Bengaluru studio reportedly built
GTA 6's Vice Beach. GTA 6 launched on 19 Nov 2026, three days ago, so the topic is live.

Runtime: 229 words. That is 92 s at 150 wpm, about 83 s at our 2.75 words a second, and about 72 s after the 1.15× VO speed-up. The reference runs 69 s.

## Fact notes (checked 22 Nov 2026)

- GTA VI launched 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (Forbes, Deadline). Take-Two reportedly gave $3.79B day-one
  sales and 32.5M copies sold before release, but I couldn't find Take-Two's own release, so it's NOT in the script.
- GTA V: 230M copies sold-in, Take-Two Q1 FY27 report (Aug 2026), via Game Developer and VGChartz.
- Vice City ~17.5M, San Andreas ~27.5M, GTA IV 25M+: the reference's figures, widely cited; said with "करीब".
- Rockstar India, Bengaluru: founded Aug 2016; Rockstar bought Dhruva Interactive (Bengaluru, founded by Rajesh Rao,
  called India's oldest game developer) in 2019 and merged it in. Earlier work on GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2.
- Vice Beach by Rockstar India and "≈1,600 developers": from news reports (The Week, Digit, NewsBytes; MP Tejasvi
  Surya's post) and partly a 2024 leak. Rockstar hasn't confirmed either, so both are hedged ("रिपोर्ट्स के मुताबिक़").
- UP population ≈ 24 crore is a projection (Census 2011: 19.98 crore). The comparison is "करीब-करीब", but swap it if
  you prefer a firmer one.
