# Sources and credits

## NASA globe

NASA Earth Observatory / Blue Marble Next Generation, July 2004, topography and bathymetry, 21,600 × 10,800. NASA imagery is used as the public-domain source specified in the brief. Original bytes are distributed as two binary pieces and restored by the included script.

https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/topography-and-bathymetry/

Exact source URL is recorded in `map_metadata.json`.
