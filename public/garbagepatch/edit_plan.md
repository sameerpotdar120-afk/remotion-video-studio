# Garbage Patch: edit plan (pace matched to the reference)

**What the reference does:** in 60 s it puts about 55 separate events on screen, so something new arrives every 1.1 s on average. The camera moves all the time, but it is never the only thing happening. The longest stretch with nothing new is 2.7 s (the five rings pulsing on the tilted card). Small props cross the frame even in the background (a plane's shadow over Alaska at 1 s and 31–40 s, boats bobbing).

**Our rule:** the VO runs about 97 s. Plan a hit at least every 1.2 s (about 90 in total), with no gap over 1.5 s. A hit is a prop popping in, a text pop, a fill or outline, a punch-zoom, a cut or a transition. Every VO noun the viewer can picture gets its own prop on screen at the moment it's said.

Times are estimates at 2.6 words per second and get re-timed to the real VO. **gpNN** is an image prompt (`asset_prompts.md`) and **pNN** is a Remotion motion piece (`motion_assets.md`).
