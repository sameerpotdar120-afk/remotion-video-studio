# Reference analysis: "Invisible Island in the Pacific Ocean | The Great Pacific Garbage Patch"

Source: 720×1280, 30 fps, 60.44 s. Checked at 2 fps across the whole video, then at 12.5 fps (every 2–3 frames) through each transition: the intro push-in, the world zoom-out at 21 s, the cut into real footage at 44.5 s, the cut back out at 47.5 s and the smear wipe at 53 s.

## Look

- **Map:** a bright, flat, top-down satellite map (Blue Marble style: green land, white ice, teal shelf, deep blue ocean). No tilt or relief, except one tilted "map card" at 22–30 s. We rebuild it from EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless plus Natural Earth, like the Judwa Desh look.
- **Captions:** small white bold sans, centred at about 69% of frame height, one short phrase at a time, with no box. They change every 1–2 s.
- **Accent text:** bigger white or cyan words above the hero object, with a soft glow ("Missing from any map", "TRASH", "No one lives there", "617,000 mi²", "Microplastics").
- **Palette:** ocean teal-blue, cyan (#3fd8ff) for outlines and numbers, gold (#ffc21a) for the California highlight, pink, lime and blue country fills, and a pink-red glow for the food-chain icons.
- **Camera:** it never stops. There is a slow constant push-in, or drift, on every beat, plus a long zoom-out to the world and a zoom back into the Pacific.

## Transitions used

1. Continuous push-in on the map, with no cuts (0–20 s).
2. A zoom-out to the world, then a 3D tilt into a perspective card on a grid background (21–22 s).
3. A dive back in through a numbered ring, with a foreground ring flying past for depth (25–26 s).
4. A pixel mosaic resolving into the real image (7.4 s).
5. A dissolve into a speckle texture (36 s).
6. A **radial zoom blur in and out** for the real footage (44.5 s and 47.6 s), matching textures across the cut.
7. A liquid smear wipe (53 s) into a flat cartoon scene.

## Sound

- **BGM:** a soft, sustained tonal bed (an ambient pad or soft piano) under the VO, about −36 dB RMS in the pauses. The spectral peaks sit at 110, 220 and 440 Hz, with some energy near 208 Hz, which suggests a calm loop around A. The bed is constant, with no drops. It lifts slightly from 48 to 51 s (the "vortex" beat).
- **SFX:** low-frequency whoosh or impact hits on the big moves: 17 s (country shapes land), 21–22 s (zoom-out and card tilt), 26 s (dive back in), **44 s (the strongest hit, the zoom blur into the footage)**, 48 s (zoom out of the footage) and 53 s (the smear). Under the VO there are small high-frequency ticks and pops for the letter type-ons, the bubble pops and the ring pops.
- **VO:** a single English narrator, fast and even (about 2.4 words per second), with no pauses longer than 0.6 s.

## Facts check (for our script)

- The reference says "617,000 mi² (992,965 km²)". The mi² figure matches the 2018 estimate (about 1.6 million km²), but the km² sub-line on screen is wrong. We use **about 1.6 million km² (16 लाख वर्ग किलोमीटर)**, which is about half of India (3.287 million km²).
- Lebreton et al. 2018 (*Scientific Reports*): about 1.6 million km², about 80,000 tonnes, about 1.8 trillion pieces, with microplastics making up most of the pieces but only about 8% of the mass. Fishing nets are a large share of the mass.
- "Five zones": the five big subtropical gyres, and the North Pacific one is the largest.
