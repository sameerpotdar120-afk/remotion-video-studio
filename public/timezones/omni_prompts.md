# Time zones: Omni prompts for Null

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** `null_tz_A1.mp4`, `null_tz_A2.mp4` and so on (2–3 takes).

---

**Clip A** (end, about 9 s: "और Nepal ने तो और भी मज़ेदार काम किया, अपनी घड़ी India से सिर्फ़ 15 मिनट आगे कर ली।" / "तो बताओ, क्या India को भी 2 time zones की ज़रूरत है? हाँ या ना, comment करो।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He shares a funny fact, then asks the audience a question:
0–3 s: he raises his left wrist and taps it twice with his right index finger, as if checking a watch, then his shoulders bounce in a small laugh.
3–6 s: he holds up two fingers toward the camera, as if saying "two", and tilts his head with curiosity.
6–10 s: he points one finger toward the camera, then turns both palms up in an open "what do you think?" gesture and rests his hands beside the microphone.

Smooth, friendly, explanatory movements, with his hands staying near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
