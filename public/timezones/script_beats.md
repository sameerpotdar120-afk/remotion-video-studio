## Fact notes (checked 18 October 2026)

- **"24 time zones":** that's the textbook figure for hourly zones. Real offsets number about 38, which the video doesn't claim otherwise.
- **"0 की लकीर, UTC" at Greenwich:** the prime meridian at Greenwich is the reference for UTC offsets.
- **"हमारा India? +5:30":** IST is UTC+5:30.
- **"India का time इसी शहर से गुज़रती लकीर से":** IST is calculated from 82.5°E, which passes Mirzapur (the clock tower, Ghanta Ghar, lies within about 4 angular minutes of it; Wikipedia, Mirzapur Clock Tower).
- **"सीधे zones में यही लकीर शहर को आधा-आधा काट देती":** with straight hourly zones centred on multiples of 15°, UTC+5 runs from 67.5°E to 82.5°E and UTC+6 from 82.5°E to 97.5°E, so the boundary is exactly 82.5°E, through Mirzapur. This is a what-if, as the line says.
- **France, "पूरे 12":** 12 time zones counting its overseas territories, the most of any country (13 counting its Antarctic claim). Russia and the US have 11 each (The Local, Wikipedia "Time in France").
- **China, "5 zones जितना चौड़ा, पर घड़ी एक":** five zones (UTC+5:30 to +8:30) were defined in the Republic era. They were never fully observed, and the country has used Beijing Time (UTC+8) since 1949. Many Uyghur residents of Xinjiang informally use UTC+6, which the video doesn't cover.
- **"Kashgar में सर्दियों में सुबह 10 बजे तक सूरज नहीं निकलता":** a solar calculation (NOAA method) for 39.47°N 75.99°E on 21 December gives sunrise at 10:12 Beijing time.
- **"करीब 3,000 किलोमीटर चौड़ा":** India's official east–west extent is 2,933 km.
- **"गर्मियों में Arunachal में सूरज 4 बजे… Gujarat में 6 बजे के बाद" (the draft's "6:30 तक अँधेरा" was changed):** a solar calculation for 21 June gives Dong (Arunachal) 4:05 AM IST and Guhar Moti (Kutch) 6:11 AM IST. In winter, Dong's sunrise is about 5:50, so the line says "गर्मियों में".
- **"Assam के कई चाय बागानों में … एक घंटा आगे, बागान टाइम":** tea-garden time (चाय बागान टाइम) is IST+1, introduced under British rule for daylight working hours. It's still used informally in many gardens, though it isn't legally recognised and no exact start year is documented, so the line says "अंग्रेज़ों के ज़माने से" and "कई".
- **Nepal, "15 मिनट आगे":** Nepal used IST from 1920 and moved to UTC+5:45 in 1986 (its meridian passes Gaurishankar, about 100 km east of Kathmandu).
- **The close:** India having two time zones has been proposed (Assam's government pushed for it, and there's a long-running debate). The on-screen "+6:30" split is labelled as a proposal, not as fact.
- **India:** shown with its official boundary (Natural Earth, India point-of-view edition). Time zone boundaries come from Natural Earth's public-domain time zone layer.
