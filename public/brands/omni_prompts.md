# Brands: Omni prompts for Null

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach
`public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** the file name below (2–3 takes, e.g. `null_br_A1.mp4`, `null_br_A2.mp4`).

The video loops, so Null appears once, in the middle, for about 4–5 s.

---

**null_br_A.mp4** (beat 3, the flip: "लेकिन खेल उल्टा भी है। जिन brands के नाम बिल्कुल विदेशी लगते हैं, वो असल में देसी निकलते हैं।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He reveals a surprising twist:
0–2 s: he raises one hand in front of the microphone, palm facing down, and holds it there for a moment.
2–4 s: with a small, confident snap of the wrist he turns the hand over so the palm faces up, as if flipping something upside down, and his eyebrows rise.
4–7 s: he leans in slightly toward the microphone with a knowing half-smile and a slow single nod, as if saying "yes, really".
7–10 s: he lowers the hand and rests both hands on the desk, still smiling.

Smooth, natural, explanatory movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
