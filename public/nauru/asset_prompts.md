# Nauru Short: asset prompts (your GPT images), in the reference video's style

Style as in the reference: cute chibi cartoon characters (big round head merged into a short bean-shaped body,
no legs, tiny arms, dot eyes, soft 3D-ish shading) placed into photoreal scenes and next to photoreal props. The
characters are our own designs: their looks, clothes and faces are not copied from the reference's president or
police chief.

The maps (Delhi airport, the globe, Nauru from above) come from code: EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless (CC BY 4.0, credited on
screen) and Natural Earth (India's official boundary). Icons, stamps, counters, speech bubbles, flags and the route
lines are drawn in code too.

Paste the style line at the start of every prompt. All vertical 9:16. Where it says CUT-OUT, also make a version on
a plain flat mid-grey background, full figure, nothing cropped, so we can place it on the map. Every image gets an
"AI illustration" label on screen.
