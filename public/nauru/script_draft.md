# Nauru: Hindi Short (draft v1, 22 Nov 2026)

Reference: an 85.6 s English Short (720×1280, 30 fps). Nauru's rise and fall, told on a satellite map with
cartoon sketches in between (a president handing out houses and Lamborghinis), stamps and icons (FREE, NO TAXES,
BROKE) and a closing citizenship-for-sale twist. The reference's script and characters belong to that creator. This
is our own script, built from our own fact check (below), with an India comparison at the core.

India angle: Nauru is 21 km². Delhi's IGI airport is 20.66 km² (5,106 acres). A whole country, about the size of
Delhi airport.

## Script (hook A). VO in Devanagari, numbers as words for the voice; digits on screen.

## Fact notes (checked 22 Nov 2026)

- Area: Nauru 21 km² (1987 inquiry; Wikipedia). Delhi IGI airport 2,066 ha / 5,106 acres ≈ 20.66 km² (Wikipedia; one
  outlet says 6,300 acres, so the video says "जितना", about the same).
- Third-smallest country by area (after Vatican City and Monaco): widely listed. Independence 1968.
- 1975 per-capita GDP ≈ $50,000, second only to Saudi Arabia: Helen Hughes' estimate (via devpolicy.org). Another
  source gives $35,700 and "4.5× the US"; figures differ, so the video says "एक अनुमान के मुताबिक़" and does not use
  the reference's "twice as rich as America".
- No income tax and near-free services in the boom years: widely reported. Nauru brought in an income tax only in
  2014, so "उस दौर में" is implied by the timeline.
- Air Nauru, 1983: seven aircraft (two Boeing 727-100s, five 737-200s); population about 8,000 (Wikipedia, citing
  period sources). 8,000 / 7 ≈ 1,143, said as "हर ग्यारह सौ लोगों पर".
- Phosphate from seabird droppings: the common account; some sources just say phosphate rock, so "माना जाता है".
- More than 80% of the island devastated: 1987 government inquiry. CIA World Factbook: about 90% unsuitable for
  farming.
- Near bankrupt by about 2000 (offshore-banking phase ended 2005; large-scale mining ended 2006).
- Australia funds Nauru to host its regional processing centre for asylum seekers (since 2012, ongoing).
- Citizenship by investment: launched at $105,000; current standard contribution $115,000 plus fees, with
  temporary discounts. The video says "एक लाख डॉलर से ज़्यादा".
- Dropped from the reference: the police chief's Lamborghini (single light source, no name or date) and "twice
  as rich as America".
