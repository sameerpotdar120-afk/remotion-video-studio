# Nauru Short: Omni prompts for Null (two short clips)

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds.

**Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`.

**Save as:** the file name above each clip, in `public/nauru/null/`. Make 2 takes each if you can, e.g.
`null_nr_A1.mp4`, `null_nr_A2.mp4`.

Null appears in the middle only, in split screen, for about 3 s each (about 6–7 s in total). He never appears at the
start or the end, because the video loops.

**null_nr_A.mp4** (beat 5, the line "Yaani har gyarah sau logon par ek Jet. Matlab Delhi se Patna ki kareeb chhah bhari flights jitne log...")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He can't believe a tiny country bought so many planes:
0–3 s: he counts on his fingers, one hand tapping the fingers of the other, slowly, as if adding something up.
3–5 s: he holds one flat hand out low and sweeps it up and away diagonally, like a plane taking off.
5–8 s: he leans back, slaps one hand to his forehead and shakes his head in disbelief.
8–10 s: he rests both hands calmly on the desk.

Clear, deliberate movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```

```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He counts slowly on his fingers, sweeps a flat hand up and away like a plane taking off, slaps his forehead and shakes his head in disbelief, then rests his hands.
```

**null_nr_B.mp4** (beat 8, the line "Paisa aaya, to use bachane ke bajaye udana shuru kar diya.")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He shows money being blown instead of saved:
0–3 s: he mimes holding a thick stack of banknotes in one hand and flicking through it with the thumb of the other.
3–5 s: he tosses the imaginary stack up into the air with both hands, fingers spread, as if the notes are flying everywhere.
5–8 s: he watches them fall, then gives a slow, regretful shrug with both palms up.
8–10 s: he rests both hands calmly on the desk.

Natural, expressive movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```

```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He flicks through an imaginary stack of banknotes, tosses it into the air with fingers spread, watches it fall, gives a regretful shrug with palms up, then rests his hands.
```
