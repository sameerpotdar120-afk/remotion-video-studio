Everest ki choti par samundar ke jeevon ke fossil milte hain. Aur wo patthar kabhi Bharat ka samundar tha.
Tum Everest ki choti par khade ho, to tum asal mein ek purane samundar ke tal par khade ho.
Duniya ki sabse oonchi jagah, 8,849 meter, kabhi samundar ke neeche thi.
Everest ki choti ka patthar 47 crore saal pehle Bharat ke kinare, samundar ke neeche bana tha.
Himachal ke ek gaon mein log 4,400 meter par samundar ke jeevon ke fossil dhoondhte hain, aur unka raaz Everest ki choti par hai.
