# Everest: Omni prompts for Null

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach
`public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** the file name below (2–3 takes, e.g. `null_ev_B1.mp4`, `null_ev_B2.mp4`).

The video loops seamlessly, so Null appears once, in the middle, for about 4–5 s.

---

**null_ev_B.mp4** (beat 4: "फिर भारत उत्तर की ओर खिसकता गया, और क़रीब पाँच करोड़ साल पहले एशिया से जा टकराया।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He explains two huge pieces of land colliding and a mountain rising:
0–3 s: he holds both hands apart in front of the microphone, palms facing each other, and slowly moves one hand toward the other as if one land is drifting.
3–5 s: his hands meet and press firmly together with a small push, his shoulders tensing slightly, as if two lands crashed.
5–8 s: keeping the heels of his hands together, he slowly lifts his fingertips upward into a peak, like a mountain rising, his eyebrows rising with it.
8–10 s: he holds the peak for a moment, then lowers his hands and rests them on the desk.

Smooth, calm, explanatory movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
