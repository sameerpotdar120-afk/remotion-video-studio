# Chile: Omni prompts for Null

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** `null_chile_A1.mp4`, `null_chile_B1.mp4` and so on (2–3 takes each).

---

**Clip A** (middle, after the skit: "अहम… हाँ तो मैं कह रहा था… कितनी खूबसूरत खाली ज़मीन!")
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He reacts to something ridiculous he just heard:
0–3 s: he slowly lowers his head and brings one hand up to his forehead in a classic facepalm.
3–6 s: he lifts his head and shakes it slowly from side to side, palms open, in a "can you believe this?" gesture.
6–10 s: he spreads both hands toward the camera, as if saying "seriously?", then rests his hands on the desk.

Smooth, comedic but gentle movements, with his hands beside the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```

```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He does a slow facepalm, then shakes his head with open palms, then spreads his hands toward the camera as if saying "seriously?". Smooth, hands beside the microphone.
```

**Clip B** (end: "उस दोस्त को भेजो, जिसे लगता था Chile बस एक मिर्ची है।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He asks the viewer to share the video with a friend:
0–3 s: he points warmly at the camera with his index finger.
3–6 s: he holds up one hand as if holding a phone and taps it with his thumb a couple of times.
6–10 s: he makes a small forward flick with that hand, like sending a message, then gives a friendly thumbs-up beside the microphone.

Smooth, friendly movements, with his hands beside the microphone, never in front of his face. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
Backup:
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He points at the camera, mimes tapping a phone in one hand, flicks it forward like sending a message, then gives a thumbs-up. Smooth, hands beside the microphone.
```
