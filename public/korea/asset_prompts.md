# Korea: image prompts

Only two images. Everything else is drawn in code: the satellite map, North/South fills, the DMZ band, barbed wire,
mines and guns icons, flags (DPRK, ROK, USSR, US, UN, China, India), the 38th parallel, the stopwatch, the moving front
lines, arrows, the armistice and peace-treaty papers, the red cross tag and the counters.

Style for both: photoreal studio cut-out, soft even light, sharp detail, natural colours, 4K, plain pure white #FFFFFF
background, no shadow on the ground, no text, no logos, no watermark. The subject is BIG and fills 85–90 % of the
frame, nothing cut off at the edges. Generic people, not portraits of real persons.
