Tumhe lagta hai Korea ki jung 1953 me khatam ho gayi? Kabhi nahi hui. 73 saal, shanti par koi sign nahi, aur ek line aaj bhi parivaro ko baante hue hai. Kyu?

Beech me bas 4 kilometer ki patti: kaantedaar taar aur baaroodi surange.

Par 1945 tak ye ek hi desh tha, 35 saal se Japan ke kabze me.

Fir usi saal Soviet sena uttar se ghus aayi, aur America ko darr tha ki poora Korea haath se nikal jayega.

To do American afsaro ne map uthaya, aur kareeb aadhe ghante me ek line kheench di: 38th parallel. Yahi hai wo line.

Batwara kuch samay ke liye tha. Par Cold War me upar communism aaya, neeche capitalism, aur 1948 me do desh ban gaye.

Aur dono ka daava ek: poora Korea hamara.

1950 me North ne hamla kiya, aur lagbhag poora South jeet liya. Fir America aur UN ki fauj aayi, aur North ko China ki seema tak dhakel diya.

Tab China kooda, laakho sainiko ke saath.

[OPTIONAL] Aur Bharat? Hamari 60 Para Field Ambulance ne wahan hazaaro ghayalo ka ilaaj kiya.

3 saal, kam se kam 25 laakh maute, aur ladai lagbhag wahin ruki jahan se shuru hui thi: usi line ke paas.

1953 me yuddhviraam hua. South Korea ne us par sign tak nahi kiya. Aur shanti samjhauta? Aaj tak nahi.
