Tumhe lagta hai Korea ki jung 1953 me khatam ho gayi? Khatam hui hi nahi.

73 saal ho gaye, shanti par aaj tak koi sign nahi, aur ek line aaj bhi parivaaron ko baante hue hai. Kyu?

Beech me bas 4 kilometer ki patti hai. Kaantedar taar, baroodi surangein, aur dono taraf bandookein taani hui.

Par 1945 tak ye ek hi desh tha. 35 saal se Japan ke kabze me.

Fir usi saal Soviet sena uttar se ghus aayi, aur America ko darr tha ki poora Korea haath se nikal jayega.

To do American officers ne map uthaya, aur kareeb aadhe ghante me ek line kheench di: 38th parallel. Bas. Yahi hai wo line.

Batwara kuch samay ke liye tha. Par Cold War me upar communism aa gaya, neeche capitalism, aur 1948 me do desh ban gaye.

Aur dono ka daava ek hi tha: poora Korea humara hai.

1950 me North ne hamla kiya, aur lagbhag poora South jeet liya. Fir America aur UN ki fauj aayi, aur North ko China ki seema tak dhakel diya.

Tab China kooda, lakhon sainikon ke saath.

Aur yahin India aaya. Humne ladne ke liye fauj nahi bheji, bachane ke liye bheji. Hamari 60 Para Field Ambulance. 627 log, jinhone 2.2 lakh se zyada mareezon ka ilaaj kiya, aur 2,324 surgeries ki, goliyon ke beech me.

3 saal, kam se kam 25 lakh mautein, aur ladai lagbhag wahin ruki jahan se shuru hui thi. Usi line ke paas.

1953 me yudhviram hua. South Korea ne us par sign tak nahi kiya. Aur shanti samjhauta? Aaj tak nahi.

To ye sab jaanne ke baad bhi...
