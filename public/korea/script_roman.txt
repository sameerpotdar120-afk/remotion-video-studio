Tumhe lagta hai Korea ki jung 1953 me khatam ho gayi? Kabhi nahi hui. Bandooke ruki, par shanti par aaj tak kisi ne sign nahi kiya.

Aaj bhi na koi dootavas, na koi rishta. Beech me bas 4 kilometer ki patti: kaantedaar taar aur baaroodi surange.

Par 1945 tak ye ek hi desh tha, 35 saal se Japan ke kabze me.

1945 me Japan haar ki kagaar par tha, aur Soviet sena uttar se ghus aayi. America ko darr tha ki poora Korea haath se nikal jayega.

To do American afsaro ne kareeb aadhe ghante me map par ek line kheench di: 38th parallel.

Batwara kuch samay ke liye tha. Par Cold War me upar communism aaya, neeche capitalism, aur 1948 me do desh ban gaye.

Dono ka daava ek hi: poora Korea hamara.

1950 me North ne hamla kiya, aur lagbhag poora South jeet liya.

Fir America aur UN ki fauj aayi, aur North ko China ki seema tak dhakel diya.

Tab China kooda, laakho sainiko ke saath.

3 saal aur kam se kam 25 laakh mauto ke baad, ladai lagbhag wahin ruki jahan se shuru hui thi.

1953 me yuddhviraam hua. South Korea ne us par sign tak nahi kiya. Aur shanti samjhauta? Aaj tak nahi.
