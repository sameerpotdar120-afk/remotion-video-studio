# Reference breakdown: "Top Safest Countries for World War 3" (#4 Fiji)

Source: the creator's reference, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 53.9 s, English VO. Analysed every 2–3 frames through each motion
(frame numbers below are the reference's 30 fps frames; times in seconds). Nothing from it is reused: no footage,
map tiles, VFX, icons or audio. We rebuild every look from our own sources.

## Constant layers

- **Subtitles:** 2–4 word phrases, white bold rounded sans (Poppins-like) with a thin dark outline and shadow, centred
  at about 62% of the height. No animation; a hard switch per phrase.
- **Watermark:** grey channel name at the bottom left.
- **Grade:** teal satellite map (cyan-teal ocean, warm land), a soft vignette, no film grain.
- **Audio:** the level never drops to silence (a floor of about −30 dB), so there's a music bed under the VO the whole
  way through, plus dense short hits at transitions.

### 1. Missiles and red fills (0–3.9 s)

- **Missile head:** a white-hot core (about 10 px) inside a big yellow glow (about 40 px).
- **Trail:** a white spray trail behind the head, tapered and slightly fuzzy, like particles rather than a clean line.
  It's about a third of the path long. Paths are gentle arcs, and each flight takes 0.8–1.0 s at nearly constant speed.
  Several fly at once, staggered by 4–8 frames.
- **Impact:** the target country fades to about 50% red over 6 frames with a red outer glow and a bright red edge, pulses
  once (brighter, then settles) and holds. Order: France, then Poland/Ukraine/Belarus, Finland/Norway, then Spain.

### 2. Title banner (frames 64–117)

- A black box with white text plus a pink-red highlight ("World War III Begins?").
- **Frame 64:** it enters from off-screen top-left at about 3× scale and −12°.
- **Frames 64–82 (0.6 s):** it shrinks to about 1.1× and rotates to −4° on a strong expo ease-out.
- **Frames 82–105:** it settles to 1× and 0°, pinned under the top edge. It holds until the cut.

### 3. Map → globe (frames 113–129)

- A fast zoom-out with radial motion blur. The flat map bends into a sphere and drops from full frame to about 45% of the
  width.
- The space background (a dark teal-green nebula with sparse stars) fades in behind it.
- The landing is ease-out, with no bounce.

### 4. Globe in space (frames 129–205)

- **Globe:** a cyan atmosphere rim glow, a night-side shadow on the left limb, and a slow rotation (a few degrees a second).
- **Camera:** a constant gentle push-in (about 45% → 55% of the width over 1.2 s).

### 5. Nukes (frames 205–290)

Three thin white arcs, each with a yellow head, launch over the top limb. Each impact goes:

- **Frames 1–3:** a white flash puff.
- **Frames 3–12:** an orange-yellow fireball blooms with a slight overshoot.
- **Frames 12–60:** it rises into a mushroom cap on a dark stem and darkens to brown-black smoke.
- **Frames 60–90:** it drifts up and dissolves.

The clouds stand on the globe's horizon while the globe keeps turning under them (Europe → Asia, about 40° in 2.5 s).

### 6. Globe spin (frames 290–510)

- The globe turns east through Asia to the Pacific: Australia in red, Antarctica pale lilac. The safe countries pulse as
  small yellow glowing blobs (New Zealand, Fiji, then Iceland and Switzerland as it comes round to Europe).
- It turns almost 360° in about 7 s, easing in and out, while the globe size breathes between 45% and 55%.

### 7. Dive to Fiji (frames 498–532)

- **Frames 498–516:** a zoom into the globe that keeps accelerating (about ×3 in 18 frames, ease-in) while it rotates
  towards the Pacific.
- **Frame 517:** a hard cut to the flat map, smeared by heavy directional blur.
- **Frames 518–532:** the pan decelerates (ease-out) and lands on Fiji.
- **Fiji:** a bright green fill with a white edge pops (0 → 1.1 → 1), then the "Fiji" label pops letter by letter
  (frames 522–530).

### 8. Distance line (frames 546–663)

- **Pull-back, frames 546–580:** the camera pulls back and pans left (ease-in-out, 1.1 s). A white dashed line draws
  from Fiji towards Australia.
- **"1,800 miles":** big bold italic white text with a soft shadow. It rides at the line's head, rotated to the line
  angle (about −25°), and its letters reveal left to right as the line grows.
- **Text anchoring:** the text is pinned to the map, so it shrinks as the camera pulls back.
- **Australia, around frame 573:** a solid red fill with a white edge and an inner glow, plus a small-caps
  "AUSTRALIA" label.
- **Return, frames 630–663:** after a hold, the camera makes the reverse move back to Fiji and the line fades.

### 9. Pacific flash (frames 694–724)

- The whole frame's colour pushes to bright saturated blue: up over 15 frames, back down over 15 (peak at frame 712).
- A cyan small-caps "PACIFIC OCEAN" label fades in under the subtitle.

### 10. Stickers on Fiji (frames 738–825)

- Slow push-in, and the map darkens about 25% so Fiji pops.
- Stickers, each 0 → 1.15 → 1 in 8–10 frames with a small tilt:
  - a "BEACH" signboard (738–748)
  - two palm trees (750–762)
  - a sun (around 760)
  - a beach ball (768–776)
- The stickers have a white cut-out edge and a soft shadow.

### 11. Whip out and blink (frames 825–870)

- A fast zoom-out with motion blur up to world scale. The camera drifts up-left to Asia.
- Asia fills pink and **blinks** on/off at 3-frame steps (on 858, off 861, on 864), then holds.

### 12. Americas and Europe (frames 870–1032)

- The camera pans right. The Americas fill (dull red first, then pink), and Europe fills too.
- Black spiky "splat" bursts with grey smoke sit on the USA and Brazil.
- Missiles cross the Atlantic (903–945). Each impact gives a yellow flash, a grey smoke puff and a black splat. The
  splats rotate slowly and keep smoking.
- The map drifts and zooms the whole time; it never sits still.

### 13. Wipe to the light map (frames 1032–1044)

- The pink fills fade out over 3 frames.
- A slanted white wipe crosses the frame (a semi-transparent white layer with a hard diagonal edge) and lands on a light
  vector world map: pale cyan ocean, off-white land, faint borders.

### 14. Resources (frames 1074–1200)

- **Dots:** about 200 dots pop in a wave out from China, then Southeast Asia and Australia. Each starts as a small pale
  pink dot and becomes a saturated red dot (#E8174A) at 0 → 1.2 → 1 in about 6 frames. Sizes vary from 8 to 22 px.
- **Fiji tag:** a green rounded "FIJI" tag with a glow pops at frame 1092. Fiji gets no dots, which is the point.
- **Camera:** it pans steadily right and down for about 4 s to North America.
- **USA:** the USA and Alaska fill flat periwinkle blue (#7B93F2) through a mask that grows outward from each part
  (ease-out, 1.2 s).

### 15–16. Live footage (frames 1209–1350)

- A push-slide transition: the new shot shoves the map off to the left in 6 frames with motion blur.
- Two drone shots, each with a slow push: green fields by the sea, then a beach. They change on a hard cut.

### 17. People icon (frames 1353–1494)

- A hard cut back to the darker Fiji map with a slow zoom.
- A white three-person icon pops on the main island (0 → 1.15 → 1, frames 1410–1419).

### 18. Teaser and like button (frames 1497–1617)

- A hard cut to an Asia/Africa map with a slow push-in.
- **Teaser:** blue fills flicker across many countries (Australia, China, the Middle East, India, Indonesia, parts of
  Africa). Each fades in over about 6 frames, holds about 6 and fades out, so the viewer can't tell which three are next.
- **Like button:**
  - **Frame 1575:** a blue 6-ray sparkle burst.
  - **Frames 1584–1593:** a white thumbs-up pops (0 → 1.2 → 1) with a small wobble while red and blue confetti
    dashes fly out radially.

## Our build plan for this style

Here, in Remotion (my side):
- **EOX map:** the flat Sentinel-2 map with the camera and continuous drift. Country fills use this reference's
  palette: red for war, green for safe, blue for the teaser.
- **On the map:** missile arcs with spray trails and impact pulses, the blink, and the dashed distance line with its
  map-pinned label.
- **Globe:** our own orthographic globe from the EOX world plate, with the atmosphere rim and night side, spun and dived
  in code.
- **Light map:** drawn from Natural Earth vectors (public domain). No Mapbox or Carto tiles.
- **Resources:** the dot field and the USA mask fill.
- **Transitions:** whip blur, diagonal wipe and push-slide.
- **Finishing:** the colour flash, the subtitles, the mix and the render.

## Claims to check before we use them

- **"1,800 miles from Australia":** this depends on the points measured; Suva to Brisbane is roughly 2,700 km. We'll
  measure it ourselves.
- **"Fiji lacks strategically important resources":** too strong. Fiji mines gold (Vatukoula), so we need softer
  wording.
- **"well-developed":** Fiji is an upper-middle-income country, so the wording needs care.
