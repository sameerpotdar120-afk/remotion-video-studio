# World War 3 safe countries: Omni prompts for Null

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** `null_ww3_A1.mp4`, `null_ww3_B1.mp4` and so on (2–3 takes each).

---

**Clip A** (middle: "ये तो उत्तर में है, फिर भी लिस्ट में है। क्यों?")
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He reacts to a surprising fact and then gets ready to explain it:
0–3 s: he points his right index finger upward, beside the microphone, as if pointing to the top of a map.
3–6 s: he tilts his head slightly and opens both palms in a puzzled shrug.
6–10 s: he raises one finger in a clear "here is the reason" gesture and leans in a little toward the camera.

Smooth, expressive movements, with his hands beside the microphone, never in front of his face. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```


**Clip B** (end: "और एक चौथा देश भी है… पहचाना कौन सा? Comment में नाम लिखो।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He teases a riddle and invites the viewer to answer:
0–3 s: he holds up four fingers of his right hand beside the microphone and nods once.
3–6 s: he rests his chin on his fist in a thoughtful "can you guess?" pose.
6–10 s: he points warmly at the camera with his index finger, then makes a small writing motion in the air, inviting a comment.

Smooth, friendly movements, with his hands beside the microphone, never in front of his face. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
Backup:
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He holds up four fingers, rests his chin on his fist as if thinking, then points at the camera and makes a small writing motion in the air. Smooth, hands beside the microphone.
```
