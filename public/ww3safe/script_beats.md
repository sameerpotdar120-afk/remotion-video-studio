## Fact notes (checked October 2026)

- **"सारे 9 परमाणु देश … उत्तरी हिस्से में":** the nine nuclear-armed states are the US, Russia, China, France, the UK,
  India, Pakistan, Israel and North Korea. All lie north of the equator. "बम भी वहीं गिर सकते हैं" is a hedged forecast.
- **"2 देश दक्षिण के, 1 उत्तर का":** Fiji and New Zealand are in the southern hemisphere; Iceland is in the northern.
- **Fiji, "300 से ज़्यादा द्वीप":** about 330 islands.
- **Fiji, "Australia से करीब 2,700 किलोमीटर":** measured great-circle distances are Nadi to Brisbane about 2,720 km and
  Suva to Brisbane about 2,800 km. The reference's "1,800 miles" (2,900 km) is close; we use our own km figure.
  New Zealand (Auckland to Suva, about 2,110 km) is actually nearer than Australia, so the line doesn't call Australia
  "the nearest".
- **Fiji and the treaty:** the Treaty of Rarotonga (1985) made the South Pacific Nuclear Free Zone, and Fiji is a party.
- **New Zealand, 1987:** the New Zealand Nuclear Free Zone, Disarmament, and Arms Control Act 1987.
- **The 2023 study:** Wilson and Boyd, *Risk Analysis* (University of Otago, Feb 2023). It modelled 38 island nations
  under nuclear-winter crop models. Australia, New Zealand, Iceland, the Solomon Islands and Vanuatu could produce
  enough food for their own people. The authors warn that fuel imports and the collapse of industry could still
  threaten that. The line says "उगा सकता है", not "बच जाएगा".
- **Iceland, "कोई army नहीं":** Iceland has no standing army (it has a Coast Guard). It is a founding NATO member, which
  the video doesn't claim otherwise.
- **Iceland, "19 साल से सबसे शांत":** Global Peace Index 2026 ranks Iceland first, its 19th year in a row (since 2008);
  one outlet says 18th, but most say 19th.
- **Iceland, "90 प्रतिशत घर":** Samorka (Iceland's utility association) says 90% of housing is heated directly by
  geothermal water. Older government material says about 85%.
- **Hint, "हर इंसान के लिए bunker में जगह":** Switzerland. A 2017 federal figure put public and private shelter
  spaces at 110% of the population; coverage is uneven between cantons. The answer goes in the pinned comment after
  the guesses come in.
- **Dropped from the reference:**
  - "Fiji lacks strategically important resources": Fiji mines gold.
  - "almost unlimited supply of seafood" and the self-sufficient farming claim: Fiji imports much of its food.
