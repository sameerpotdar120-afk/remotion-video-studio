Tum Ram Setu par chalkar Sri Lanka tak ja sakte the. Rameshwaram mandir ke record kehte hain, 1480 tak. Phir ek toofan ne ise tod diya, aur 1964 mein samundar ek poora shehar nigal gaya. Kaise?

Ye hai Bharat ka aakhri sira, Dhanushkodi. Matlab, dhanush ka sira. Kehte hain, Lanka se laut kar Shri Ram ne yahin apne dhanush ki nok se Setu ko toda tha. Isiliye is jagah ka naam pada Dhanushkodi.

Yahin se Sri Lanka ke Mannar tak, samundar ke neeche ek lakeer jaati hai.

Ek taraf Palk ki khaadi, doosri taraf Mannar ki khaadi, aur beech mein Ram Setu.

Aastha kehti hai, ise Shri Ram ki Vanar Sena ne banaya tha. Valmiki Ramayan mein ise Nal ne banaya. Vishwakarma ke bete Nal. Kehte hain, unke chhoone se patthar tairne lagte the. Aur Vanaron ne un pattharon par Ram naam likhkar, paanch din mein Lanka tak ka rasta bana diya. Sita Maa ko wapas laane ke liye.

Angrezon ke nakshon mein iska naam pada, Adam's Bridge. Par hamare liye ye hamesha se Ram Setu tha, hai, aur rahega.

Phir 2024 mein ISRO ne NASA ke satellite ke laser se ise pehli baar poora naapa. Lambai? 48 nahi, kareeb 29 km. Iska 99.98% hissa paani ke neeche hai, aur samundar ke tal se kareeb 8 meter upar utha hua.

Ye choona-patthar aur ret ke teele hain, aur ISRO ke mutaabik, Dhanushkodi aur Mannar ka hi samundar ke neeche wala hissa.

Vigyan ne iski lambai naapi hai, iski gehraai naapi hai. Par aastha ke liye iska jawab naap-tol mein nahi. Jo rasta Ram naam se bana, wo hazaron saal baad bhi samundar ke neeche tika hua hai.

Par Dhanushkodi ki kahani mein koi shak nahi. 22 December 1964 ki raat, ek chakravaat yahan toota.

Kareeb 115 logon se bhari ek poori train samundar mein beh gayi. Poore Pamban dweep par kareeb 1,800 log maare gaye.

Sarkar ne Dhanushkodi ko rehne layak nahi maana. Aaj wahan bas toota church, toota station, aur kuch machhuaare parivaar.

Samundar ne shehar ko mita diya, par Setu ko nahi mita paaya.

Aur usi samundar ke neeche, aaj bhi ye 29 km ki lakeer chupchaap leti hai. Kehte hain, kabhi...
