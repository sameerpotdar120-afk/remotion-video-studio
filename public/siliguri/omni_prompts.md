# Siliguri Corridor: Omni prompts for Null

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds. **Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`. **Save as:** `null_siliguri_A1.mp4`, `null_siliguri_B1.mp4` and so on (2–3 takes each).

---

**Clip A** (middle: "पर India भी तैयार है।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He reassures the viewer with confidence:
0–3 s: he raises his right hand, palm open toward the camera, in a calm "wait, listen" gesture.
3–6 s: he gives a firm, confident nod and closes his hand into a relaxed fist, resting it on the desk.
6–10 s: he gives a small thumbs-up beside the microphone, then rests both hands on the desk.

Smooth, confident movements, with his hands beside the microphone, never in front of his face. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
Backup:
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He raises an open palm in a calm "listen" gesture, gives a confident nod, then a small thumbs-up beside the microphone. Smooth, hands beside the microphone.
```

**Clip B** (end: "तो Chicken's Neck India की कमज़ोरी है या ताकत? Comment में एक शब्द लिखो।")
```
Animate this stylised 3D character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He asks the viewer to choose between two options:
0–3 s: he holds out his left hand, palm up, beside the microphone, as if presenting the first option.
3–6 s: he holds out his right hand, palm up, as if presenting the second option, then moves both hands slightly up and down like a weighing scale.
6–10 s: he points warmly at the camera with his index finger, then makes a small writing motion in the air, inviting a comment.

Smooth, friendly movements, with his hands beside the microphone, never in front of his face. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
Backup:
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He presents one open palm, then the other, weighing them like a scale, then points at the camera and makes a small writing motion in the air. Smooth, hands beside the microphone.
```
