# Baikonur: Omni prompts for Null (two short clips)

**Tool:** Google Omni (Gemini Omni Flash), vertical 9:16, 10 seconds.

**Starting frame:** attach `public/null/studio_master_9x16.jpg`.

**Save as:** the file name above each clip. Make 2 takes each if you can, e.g. `null_bk_A1.mp4`, `null_bk_A2.mp4`.

Null appears in the middle only, in split screen, for about 3 s each (about 6–7 s in total). He never appears at the
start or the end, because the video loops.

**null_bk_A.mp4** (the line "Phir 1991 mein Soviet Union toot gaya…")
```
Animate this stylised 3D animated character as a friendly podcast host in his studio. Keep the image, character design, studio, microphone, lighting and framing exactly as they are, with a static camera.

He shows something big breaking apart:
0–3 s: he holds both hands together in front of him, fingers interlocked, as if holding one big object.
3–5 s: he pulls his hands apart sharply and spreads his fingers wide, as if it just broke into pieces.
5–8 s: he raises his eyebrows and gives a slow, surprised half-nod.
8–10 s: he rests both hands calmly on the desk.

Clear, deliberate movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
Backup:
```
Animate this 3D animated podcast host. Static camera, same studio. He holds his hands together with fingers interlocked, pulls them apart sharply with fingers spread as if something broke, raises his eyebrows with a slow nod, then rests his hands.
```

**null_bk_B.mp4** (the line "Par Russia itni door kyun aaya?")

```
8–10 s: he rests both hands calmly on the desk.

Natural, expressive movements, with his hands near the microphone. His face design stays the same throughout. No dialogue, no text.
```
