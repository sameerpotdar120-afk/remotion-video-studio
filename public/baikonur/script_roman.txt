Rakesh Sharma jis jagah se antriksh gaye the, uska kiraya Russia aaj bhi har saal 115 million dollar bharta hai.

Wo jagah hai Kazakhstan ke registaan mein ye gol ghera: Baikonur Cosmodrome, duniya ka pehla Spaceport.

1955 mein Soviet Union ne ise yahan banaya, jab Kazakhstan khud USSR ka hissa tha.

Yahin se 1957 mein pehla satellite Sputnik gaya, 1961 mein pehle insaan Yuri Gagarin, aur 1984 mein Bharat ke Rakesh Sharma.

Phir 1991 mein Soviet Union toot gaya, aur ye Spaceport raaton-raat ek doosre desh mein aa gaya.

To Russia ne ise kiraye par le liya: har saal kareeb 115 million dollar, 2050 tak. Aur aaj is ghere ke andar zyadatar Russia ka kanoon aur Russia ka Rouble chalta hai.

Par Russia itni door kyun aaya? Kyunki Baikonur Russia ke zyadatar launch sites se kaafi south mein hai, Equator ke zyada kareeb. Wahan Dharti zyada tez ghoomti hai, to rocket ko muft ka dhakka milta hai, aur orbit tak kam fuel lagta hai.

Aur agar koi pooche ki registaan ka ye ghera itna khaas kyun hai, to bas itna kehna...
