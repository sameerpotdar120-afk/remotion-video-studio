Rakesh Sharma jis jagah se antriksh gaye the, uska kiraya Russia aaj bhi har saal 115 million dollar bharta hai.
Russia har saal 115 million dollar sirf ek gol ghere ka kiraya deta hai, aur wo ghera Russia mein hai hi nahi.
Tum Kazakhstan ka map dekho, beech mein ek gol ghera dikhega, jo Kazakhstan ka hokar bhi Kazakhstan ka nahi hai.
Kazakhstan ke naqshe ke beech ek gol ghera hai, jahan Kazakhstan ka nahi, Russia ka kanoon chalta hai.
Duniya ka pehla insaan jis jagah se antriksh gaya, wo jagah aaj kiraye par chal rahi hai.
