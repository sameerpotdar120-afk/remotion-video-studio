# Baikonur: asset prompts (photoreal 3D props for the map)

The map is mine, drawn in code from free data:
- the satellite globe and the push into Kazakhstan
- the country fills, the USSR in red and its break-up
- the circle, the latitude lines and the spin speeds
- the counters, labels, stamps and flags

**GPT never draws a map, a flag or any text.** No letters, no "CCCP", no national markings on suits or rockets.

Your GPT images are only the **things that must look real**. I stand each one on its real place on the map with a contact
shadow, so ground props sit on the ground. The two people rise out of the map with a name box and "AI illustration".

## How to generate (ChatGPT / GPT image)
- One image per message. Paste the **Style Lock** once at the top of the chat.
- Square, the biggest size GPT offers (2048 px or more if it can).
- `PNG-T` means a real transparent background. If you get a checkerboard or white, reply: *"Regenerate with a truly
  transparent alpha background, not a drawn checkerboard."* Or use remove.bg / Photoroom.
- Name the files exactly as listed, zip them, and share through Google Drive ("Anyone with the link").

### Style Lock (paste once)
```
You are generating production props for a premium 3D documentary Short
about the Baikonur Cosmodrome in the steppe of Kazakhstan. Every image is a
single photorealistic 3D-rendered miniature object, like a high-end game
engine render (Unreal Engine 5 / GTA VI quality): physically based
materials, real textures, soft large sunlight from the upper left with a
warm desert tone, gentle ambient occlusion, subtle tilt-shift miniature
feel. Unless I say otherwise, the camera is a three-quarter view from about
45 degrees above, the same angle for every prop, so they all sit on one
tilted map. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no flags, no national
markings, no watermark. PNG-T means a fully transparent background with a
real alpha channel. Centre the subject with generous empty padding. Keep a
soft contact shadow directly under ground objects only, no other background.
```
