## Recurring patterns (per part)

### Patterns noted in part 1

- **Narrator host:** a stick-figure "explorer" with a round white head, black dot eyes, brown fedora with a red feather plume, tan fringed leopard-ish tunic over one shoulder, black single-line limbs. He is the connective tissue: ~30 of 124 shots are him alone on flat light grey (#c4c4c4), ~45% frame height, centred, always talking (open oval mouth), cycling a tiny gesture set every ~1–2s: arms down → index finger up → open palm / arm out to the right → two-arm shrug → pointing. His spear is either held upright, held horizontally to point, or lying on the floor at his feet.
- **Narrator interrupts scenes:** he pops into a scene in the foreground while the scene behind goes heavily Gaussian-blurred (shots 1, 44, 53, 99). He enters by rising from the bottom edge (shot 53) and leaves by sinking out bottom-left (shot 59). Sometimes he stands in the scene's world (knee-deep in flood water, shot 81; in front of a blurred city gate, 79/81).
- **Chapter roadmap device:** narrator shot → he vanishes and a tall rolled-up brown scroll on black stands appears (end of shots 6, 28, 45, 61, 74, 97, 114) → hard cut to the unrolled parchment "board-game" map: ~16 brown medallions with black silhouette icons and year tags, linked by a dotted path. Each chapter, a black dashed segment draws to the next medallion and its icon "colourises" (flag 1162 → ger 1170 → handshake 1206 → bow 1207 → fortress 1209 → letter 1218 → gears 1227). Each roadmap shot lasts ~2–2.5s and is a static hold.
- **Map look:** satellite-relief basemap (natural-colour, green/tan land, deep teal-blue ocean) with flat, opaque political fills in saturated colours and thin white borders: Mongols in lemon/golden yellow (#e2e51c / #f8d41e) (red #c8261c in the intro), Jin in purple-blue (#4a30c0), China/Song in dark red (#9e2226), Khwarazm in brown (#b07a50), Europe in mustard, steppe in bright green. Modern-country overlays are used for orientation (green "IRAN", white country borders).
- **Map characters:** rulers appear as cartoon head icons placed on their territory (Genghis with helmet and white plume, the Shah, Ogedei). Territories get faces: an angry toothy red blob (shot 4), a scheming grin on China (39), peeking eyes on Jin/Korea (83, 99). Heads move across the map to show invasions, with motion-blur smears (110). Gags include a V-sign selfie with a camera flash (92–95).
- **Map overlays:** Google-Maps-style red teardrop pins for cities (Beijing, Otrar, Karakorum). Dashed routes: red for trade routes and the Silk Road, black for roads. An illustrated grey Great Wall sprite, flame sprites on burning cities, money sacks with "$", and a handshake for diplomacy. Map shots almost always push in strongly (+50% to +170%) or pull back (−60%).
- **Text style:** one rounded handwritten/marker font everywhere. Labels are white with a thin dark outline. Words are often split around icons ("Mon | head | gol") and letters are set on a wavy, bouncing baseline ("steppe"). Labels on light fills are black. Dates are white numerals in a small dark translucent box at the top-left (1206, 1209, 1215, 1227, 1229). In-scene dates such as "1162" are large white handwriting in the sky.
- **Dialogue:** no speech bubbles (except one: "Nobody here!"). Short captions in the same white-outlined handwriting float above the speaker with a tiny tick mark. They appear one phrase at a time ("I'll unite / every tribe / on this steppe") and the key word is often enlarged ("Khan", "Peace", "Otrar"). Punchlines get a separate deflating reply line from another character.
- **Emphasis numbers:** large hollow outlined numerals plus a smaller handwritten unit: "100 miles/Day" (yellow hollow), "450 merchants" (olive hollow), "50,000,000" / "1,000,000" on map regions, "10X / 100X / 1000X / 10000X" over soldier heads.
- **Cartoon character design:** thick black outlines, flat fills, minimal shading, round heads, small dot or white-oval eyes, stick-thin black legs on clothed bodies. Genghis (adult) has a black beard, a steel helmet with a white plume, gold/blue lamellar armour and a red cape. Young Temujin has a brown fur hat and a tan deel. Hoelun has long black hair and a mustard robe. Jin soldiers have blue helmets with red plumes and grey armour. Generic Mongols wear fur hats, brown/grey robes and grey helmets. Expressions carry the jokes (wide eyes, deadpan, gritted teeth).

Night = navy #132a5c with white star specks, crescent moon and teal-green ground. Interiors = cream/tan lattice gers and red-pillar Chinese palaces. Persian palace = beige plus gold pillars. Dusk battlefield = maroon→orange gradient.

  - iris/circle vignette closing onto a portrait (10, 11)
  - diagonal split-screen push (5) and vertical split map|scene (100)
