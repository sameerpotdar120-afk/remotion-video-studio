## Description (copy everything between the lines)

The only tree for 20 miles sits beside a dead-straight, empty road… and somebody hits it every week. The town adds a giant glowing arrow. The crashes double. Even the sheriff drives straight into it. 🌳🚓

It's called target fixation: when there's nothing else to look at, your eyes lock onto the one thing that stands out, and your hands quietly steer toward it. That's why riders and drivers are taught one rule: look where you want to go.

The town, the sheriff and our tow-truck guy are a story to explain the effect; target fixation itself is real.
Learn more: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Target_fixation
