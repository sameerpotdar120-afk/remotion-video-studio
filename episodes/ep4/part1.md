# Mountain Cabin series: Part 1 "The Knock" (script)
Our own retelling of reference #8 (1991 mountain-house disappearance) as a Shorts series, in first person, with our own names (narrator, wife Emma, best friend Ryan + fiancee Kate, brother Josh + girlfriend Lauren).

## Voice: paste between the lines
---
You won't believe what happened after we opened the door to a stranger at 10 PM in the woods. Four people never came home.

It was the summer of 1991. My best friend Ryan had just gotten engaged to Kate, and they invited us up to their cabin in the mountains to celebrate. Me, my wife Emma, and my brother Josh with his girlfriend Lauren.

Josh drove us up. The cabin sat at the end of a long gravel driveway, nothing around it but pine trees for miles.

We ate, drank wine, and laughed about old times. It was perfect.

Then, around ten, someone knocked on the door.

We all froze. Ryan said he wasn't expecting anyone.

Kate opened the door, and I stood right behind her.

It was a young woman. Pale. Dark circles under her eyes. She said she and her husband had broken down, and pointed down the driveway. At the bottom sat an old pickup, hood up, and a man standing next to it, waving at us.

Ryan grabbed his toolbox and headed down to help.

The woman sat at our table. The first thing she said was, "Do you have any food?"

Kate gave her leftover chicken. She ate it like she hadn't eaten in days.

I asked how they ended up stranded right at our driveway.

She said, "My brother and I were going camping."

"Your brother? You said your husband."

She stopped chewing. And slowly looked up at me.
