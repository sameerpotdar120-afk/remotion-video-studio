# Script: The cockpit door (Germanwings 9525 vs Flydubai 1073)

Short-form reel, about 62 s at 150 wpm (155 words). Written with `/yt-script`. Script only, no video.

**Hook (75, STRONG, The Question):** Your pilot's door is built so nobody gets in. In 2015, that's exactly why 150 people were dead within minutes. What changed?

**Runner-up (59, WORKABLE, The Question):** You trust the cockpit door to keep you safe. In 2015, it locked the captain out while 150 people died. So why did it save Flydubai 1073?
