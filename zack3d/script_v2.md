## Fact fixes against the draft
- "Horseflies circle them just as much": the study measured approaches, not circling -> "Flies reach them just as often".
- "The stripes break their landing": the cause is a hypothesis; what was observed is that flies did not slow down and
  bumped off -> "They don't slow down. They bounce off."
- "as on plain horses" dropped for length; the comparison is still true (zebras vs horses, Caro et al. 2019).
